## Plus run: four new pets, from those she has beaten

- **Mini Illya, Mini Tsubakieh, Mini Mawang and Mini Lucy** join the guild counter's Pets tab, each only once her boss is beaten: Illya in land 18, Tsubakieh in land 24, the Mawang in land 80, and Lucy in land 100 on either ending. Until then they are not shown at all.
- **Halgakos says so** when one is waiting at the counter, the same quiet way she mentions the gallery.
- **What each does:**
  - Mini Tsubakieh runs in and swings her bat. Everything in the swing is hit, and what is left standing is dazed.
  - Mini Illya throws a fan of three sparkles.
  - Mini Mawang flies and breathes short tongues of dark flame.
  - Mini Lucy squirts a quick jet of water from further off.
- They cost 900 to 1,500, or 5 to 6 treats. They are never found as strays or in the village pet shops.
- Like every pet, they help against bosses but never land the last blow.

## Page brawl: difficulty, air dashes

- **Four difficulties** for the solo ladder: Easy, Normal, Hard and Brutal, picked under the fighter's card. Normal plays as before. Easy is slower and strikes once at a time. Hard and Brutal chain combos: strings of quick blows, blows into a special, a launch into a special in the air, a jump-in into the smash and an air dash into the smash. They also punish a missed blow and save the ultimate for an opening.
- **The gold mark** on a fighter's portrait shows the first letter of the hardest difficulty you've topped the ladder on with her.
- **Air dash:** in the air, tap an arrow twice quickly to dash toward or away from the other fighter, once per jump. Every fighter has her own forward dash and a backward half flip.
- **Smash out of an air dash:** press heavy during the dash to come down in a smash carried forward by it.
- **Quick taps on the arrows** are no longer missed, so fast double taps register on the ground and in the air.
- **Chikuwa lies on the floor** when she's knocked out, instead of floating above her bread. Halgakos's losing poses rest on the floor too.
- **The smash's burst** now shows the smasher's own things when she lands on the other fighter.
- **Two-device play** needs both phones on this version.
