## Seven new pranks

**Prank me** now has twenty-five to go through before any repeats. The new ones:

- Stickers of herself all over the screen.
- A breath on the glass, and a heart drawn in the mist.
- A disco: the lights go down, a mirror ball comes down, beams of colour go round.
- A bunch of balloons that bob against the top.
- A bubble of gum blown until it fills the screen and bursts.
- A fish tank, with fish, weed and bubbles.
- A red dot that she chases all over the screen and never catches. She is part cat.

As with all her pranks, each is a drawing over the top, she owns up within seconds, and a tap ends it.

## Rune's voice

Rune now sobs her way through her lines in a small, squeaky crybaby voice, in Plus run and in Page brawl. Her lines are shorter and simpler, with sniffles in them, and she speaks faster so that her pauses do not drag.

Still to come, and being built: the fifty new lands of Plus run with their forms, bosses and villages, and Page brawl's combos, movement animation, dodge and ultimate clash.

## Download

Download **Halgakos-Calculator-2.4.0.apk** below and open it on your Android device. It installs over 1.2.0 to 2.3.0. For a game between two devices, both need 2.3.0 or later.

Coming from 1.1.4 or earlier? It installs beside your old one, your saved calculations do not carry over, and the old one should be uninstalled afterwards. See the [1.2.0 notes](https://github.com/Shirochi-stack/coral-calculator/releases/tag/v1.2.0).

Requires Android 8.0 or later. This is a debug-signed preview build.

Only the APK is needed to install. `SHA256SUMS.txt` is available for optional download verification. The code of Halgakos Calculator is licensed under [MIT](https://github.com/Shirochi-stack/coral-calculator/blob/master/LICENSE), with a copy included below; the Halgakos artwork is not.
