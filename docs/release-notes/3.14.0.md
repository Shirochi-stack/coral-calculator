## Plus run: hunts at the guild hall

- **A board of hunts** stands in every guild hall, between the door and Inju's desk. Three hunts are up at a time, never two the same: beat so many of a kind of creature, beat Blot, the Margin Wyrm or the Bookmark Gecko, clear a cellar at rank A or better, or beat so many creatures one way (by landing on them, with a thrown book, with the tablet-book).
- **Only what she can do is offered:** creatures from lands open to her, and ways she has the weapon for.
- **Done over any lands.** A hunt taken on is kept as it goes, even if a land is lost. A small plate under her hearts shows each hunt that can be advanced where she is. A hunt can be given up.
- **Handed in at any guild hall.** A card says when a hunt is done, the board shows "Hand in!", and a new hunt goes up in its place. Hunts ask and pay more the more she has handed in.
- **Pay.** Pluses, the smith's materials (type metal, ink crystal, gold leaf), and a pet treat with every third hunt. The pet shop takes treats for a pet in place of pluses.
- **Milestones.** A pet of her own at 3 handed in and every sixth after; a place for one more pet of hers at 4 and 18; room for one more stray at 10 and 28. The board always says what comes next.
- **A guild rank,** with its badge, at 1, 5, 12, 22 and 35 handed in: Paper, Vellum, Leather-bound, Gilt-edged, Illuminated. It shows on the board and under the map's heading.
- **Five new lines,** four of Inju's and one of Halgakos's.

The smith's materials only pile up for now: the smithy that spends them is still to come.

Page brawl is as it was in 3.8.3.
