## Plus run: lands run into one another

- **No card between lands.** At a land's gate, "Land cleared" and its stars fade in and out mid-screen while Halgakos keeps running. Past the gate a way on leads straight into the next land: the view dims for a moment, and she comes out at its start, still running. Stars, pluses, prizes and the next land opening are kept exactly as before. Story scenes and films still come where they did.
- **Twelve ways on, one per chapter:** a bridge into a paper tree, an opening bookshelf, a rose arch, a mine with a minecart, a tunnel through a giant book, a castle gate, a printing-house lift, a path behind an ink waterfall, a rope bridge into a slime cave, a balloon tower, a sea arch with a paper boat, and a dragon-carved library door.
- **Stars without the clock:** 1 star for reaching the gate, 2 for 60% of the land's pluses, 3 for 90%. The time is no longer shown, and cellar ranks go by hurts only. Stars already earned stay.
- **The "=" gate is a teleporter:** once passed it glows and shows a Warp button. A column of light takes her to the lands, with every gate she has found outlined. A lit land puts her at its gate, any other open land starts from its beginning, and ✕ brings her back.
