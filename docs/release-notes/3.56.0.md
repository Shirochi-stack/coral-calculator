## Plus run: the Mirror Depths' shadow cast and the Echoes

- **The shadow cast:** on floors 3, 6, 9 and 12 of the Mirror Depths, a shadow of one of her friends waits, made of ink with a violet glow: Chikuwa leaps, Rune throws ink, Illya floats amid orbiting shots, Tsubakieh charges with her bat and the Mawang darts. Their touch and shots blind her, and each speaks a line in a hollow, echoing version of her own voice.
- **The Echoes:** on floors 5 and 10, a Halgakos from a timeline that ended badly: the Drowned Halgakos, the Ashen Halgakos, the Knight Who Stayed, the Last Idol, the Frozen Scholar, the Hollow Maid and the Blank Page. Each wears her own outfit and weapon, tells a little of how her world ended, in Halgakos's voice grown weary, and Halgakos answers. Beat each one to lay her to rest and keep a Rose keepsake; the mirror counts how many of the seven are at rest.
- **Glass floors:** the Mirror Depths now stand on silvered violet glass.
