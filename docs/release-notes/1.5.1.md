## One pink in every animation

The animated Halgakos's hair was a different pink in each of her animations: dusky while she idled, redder and brighter when she ran, and in between when she jumped, apologised or worked. Every animation now uses the idle's pink, so her hair no longer changes shade when she changes what she is doing.

## Animated by default

**Animated Halgakos** is now on unless you have switched it off. The still Halgakos is one switch away in her settings, and uses less battery.

## At full strength the moment she speaks

A speech bubble used to bring a faded Halgakos back over a tenth of a second. She is now at full strength the instant the bubble appears, and stays there until it has gone.
