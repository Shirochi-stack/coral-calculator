## Page brawl: no more flicker

- **Fighters no longer flicker while standing or walking.** The frames added in 3.2.0 for standing and walking were separate drawings of nearly the same pose, and their small differences flickered when they were shown in turn. Standing is now one drawing that breathes; walking uses the frames that were drawn together as one stride, with a small bob in step.
- The wind-up and follow-through of every blow, the fuller jump, and the frames for guarding, turning, being hit and getting up are kept.
- **Phones with 3 GB of memory or more always show the fighters at full size.** Only the smallest devices read them smaller, and less so than before.
- The download is about 2 MB smaller.

Plus run and the rest are as they were in 3.3.1.
