## Plus run: pick several colours at once

- **A palette in place of the arrow.** The button beside the ribbon opens a picker, with the land waiting. Every colour she owns is a toggle: tap the ones you want, several at once, and press **Wear** to put them all on together. Each shows whether it is on, waiting or ready, and the card names the mix the ticks would make.
- **Her mix is remembered.** What you wear from the picker is kept, between lands and between sessions, and one tap on the ribbon calls the whole mix again: every colour of it that is ready, passing over any still waiting. The ribbon shows the mix, with a dot for each colour.
- **Quick choices** along the top of the picker: One at a time, All I can wear, and each named mix she has made before.
- A hold on the ribbon still opens the same picker.

Everything else is as it was in 3.4.0.
