## Plus run: crowds

- **Five times the creatures in every land,** twice as many in the first three. They come in packs on the ground and the ledges, with more throwers where a land has them and more fliers from land 21. They keep clear of the start, flags, villages and doors.
- **Twenty times the creatures in a cellar.** Each wave is its troop twenty times over: a hundred and more to a wave, and four outsized ones at the end of the last. Twenty-four are in the room at once, each marked before it comes in, and the plate counts everything still to come. The rank's par time is six minutes.
- **More weapons in the cellar:** three lie on three storeys for each wave, changing by wave.
- **The book is rarer and bursts.** One land in three has a book. Where a thrown book lands it bursts in a blast of pages that hits everything near it, and never her.
- **What is bought or won applies at once.** An upgrade bought at the guild counter from a stall inside a land, and anything won in a land, used to be kept but not felt until the next land: wings gave no second jump until she had left and come back. Now a heart, a life, the second jump and the rest are hers there and then.
- **She says where the second jump comes from.** Falling into a pit a second jump would have cleared, without wings, she says the guild counter sells them; and her line on the map points at the counter when her pluses stretch to something she lacks. The upgrade is now called "Wings: a second jump".

Page brawl is as it was in 3.6.2.
