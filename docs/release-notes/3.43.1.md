## Plus run: the guild counter scrolls

- **The guild counter now scrolls** when its list is taller than the screen, such as on a phone turned on its side. Drag it up or down, or use the mouse wheel. A thin bar at the side shows there's more.
  - **Pets:** the pet buttons and the Buy button can be reached in landscape.
  - **Vanity:** every outfit is listed. Before, outfits that didn't fit on the screen were left out.
- **A drag never buys anything:** a purchase only happens on a tap.
