## Plus run: the cellar is an arena

- **Forty-six times the size.** A cellar is now 120 squares across and 43 from floor to ceiling, where it was 14 by 8. The camera follows her up, down and across it.
- **Proper platforms.** A floor and eight storeys, joined by steps to jump up through and by springs; a ledge that rides up and down the middle; spikes along the floor; bread and ink up high; and a loft in a top corner, with a chest, for whoever can fly to it. Three layouts by land: stacks, towers and terraces.
- **Waves that are announced.** "WAVE 1", "WAVE 2" and "FINAL WAVE" come up across the middle of the screen with a sting and a moment of stillness, and "Wave cleared!" between them with a breath.
- **Every wave is different.** The first sends those that walk; the second those that hop, throw, shoot and fly; the last some of everything and one outsized creature, or the Old Book where he sleeps. Each chapter's cellars use that chapter's creatures.
- **Seen coming.** A red ring closes on the place each creature will come in at, and an arrow at the screen's edge points to the nearest of what is left.
- **A hazard and a helper.** In the second wave the lights go out, but for a glow about her and a glimmer about each creature. In the last, what is on the shelves comes down, and a ribbon lies on the middle platform.
- **A rank.** S, A, B or C from the time, the hurts and the lives, on the result card, with pluses to match and a prize for the best rank the first time it is had. A cellar can be fought again for a better one.
- A life lost in a cellar puts her back at its door with the wave she was in begun again; the waves already cleared stay cleared.

## Colours

- **No more grey hair.** Two colours close to each other blend to the colour between them. Any others, and three or more, do not blend: her hair goes from one colour of the mix to the next.

Page brawl is as it was in 3.3.2.
