## Edit anywhere

Tap anywhere in the calculation to move the cursor there. Digits, operators, **⌫**, **%**, **+/−**, and **mr** then apply at the cursor instead of the end, and the answer updates as you edit.

Deleting a number's only digit leaves a `0` in its place, and the next digit you type replaces it. For example, in `2+5×3`, tap after the `5`, press **⌫** and then **7** to get `2+7×3`. An edit that would not leave a valid calculation, such as removing the `+` from `1.5+2.5`, is ignored.

## Foldables and tablets

On unfolded foldables and tablets held upright, the keys widen to fill the screen and the display spans its full width. Phones and cover screens look the same as before.

Held sideways, the keys are larger, the tool icons sit in one row above the display, and the display and keypad stay either side of the centre.

Folding, unfolding, or rotating no longer restarts the calculator, so the cursor, full screen, and any open dialog stay as they were.

## Long calculations

A calculation that is wider than the display now fades out at the edge instead of being cut through a digit. Swipe it sideways to see the rest, and tap to edit there.
