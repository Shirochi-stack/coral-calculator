## Plus run: readable damage numbers and colour skills

- **Damage numbers you can read:** quick hits on the same creature now add up into one counting number instead of piling on top of each other. Numbers lean apart and rise at their own pace, no more than eight show at once, and big ones are shortened (1.2k, 15k).
- **Lasting ribbons,** at the counter's Colours tab: three levels that make her ribbons last a fifth, two fifths and seven tenths longer. The black ribbon never lasts longer.
- **Eighteen colour skills in the tree,** each working in its own colour, as its card says:
  - **Passives:** red blows burn, blue blows chill (and she swims better), brown adds defence, green lifts her faster, yellow quickens her attacks, purple can shrug off hurts, and white adds attack and makes forms last longer.
  - **Mixes:** Steamcraft, Comet Trail, Tidecaller and Gale Rider.
  - **Actives:** Crimson Burst, Tidal Wave, Stoneskin, Gale Force, Lightning Step and Looking Glass.
  - **The Monochrome keystone:** one ribbon at a time, but it lasts twice as long and she hits 30% harder.
