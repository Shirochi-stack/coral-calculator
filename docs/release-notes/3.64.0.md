## Plus run: what the ground pound breaks

- **Cracked floors** that only a ground pound breaks: cracked paper, slate, old boards or ice by where she is, with fissures and a glint now and then. Stronger cracks are wider and deeper and need a stronger pound; a weak one only clangs.
- **What lies under them**: vaults with two chests and a rare find, long shortcut tunnels, basements with creatures and a chest at the end, and in dungeons a hidden cache that may hold a blessing or a relic. Every one has a ladder and a hatch back up, and counts as a hidden place found.
- **The break**: chunks thrown out, dust, an impact burst, a shake, a crunch, and a "Crack!".
