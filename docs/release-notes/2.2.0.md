## Page brawl: a fighting game with the webtoon's five

A second recommended game sits under Plus run in **Play with Halgakos**: **Page brawl**, a fighting game over the whole screen. Its five fighters are drawn as they are in the webtoon's full-size pages, each from her own artwork, with frames for standing, walking, jumping, guarding, three kinds of attack, an ultimate, being hit, going down and winning.

Pick one and climb a ladder of the other four, best of three rounds each, with a few spoken words between the two before every fight.

- **Halgakos**, mid range. Her ring of API keys up close; a scan from Glossarion; and for her ultimate a chunked translation that cuts the whole stage three times, with her glossary and honorifics on show. "I've got plenty of keys. Next key Sloppy~♡"
- **Chikuwa**, close and heavy, with the most health. A baguette, the brick bread that knocks down, a bread roll lobbed, and a brick bread dropped from above. "Over here! I've got bread, nyam."
- **Tsubakieh**, close and the fastest. Normal No. 2: a poke, a full swing, the bat thrown so that it comes back, and a home run. "Caught you. Normally."
- **Rune**, long range. Ink flicked and wept, a paper knife thrown, and a flood of tears along the ground. "The only one allowed to compliment Halgakos-nim... is me♡"
- **Illya**, all range. Hearts, pink magic, a heart beam, and a grand compliment in four pulses. "Just as planned♡"

Landing and taking hits fills a meter; when it is full the special button becomes her ultimate, with a cut-in of its own. Each fighter has ten voiced lines in the same voices as in Plus run, and there are 22 sound effects. Wins and how far up the ladder each fighter has got are kept.

The fighters, stages and effects were drawn by an image model through Glossarion from the webtoon's own character art, on transparent backgrounds.
