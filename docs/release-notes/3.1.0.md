## Plus run: doors, quests, colours to keep, and seven more bosses

- **Every land has more to it.** Lands 1 to 22 are rebuilt with shape, a village and people in them, like the lands after them.
- **Doors.** They lead off the land to hidden stashes, secret passages behind a crack you break, cellars that shut and fill with three waves, and a room where pluses rain for ten seconds.
- **Side quests.** Villagers ask favours from land 4 on: three rice cakes to find, a cellar to clear, a letter to carry unhurt, a race to the next flag, a hidden room to find. The quest is tracked on screen and sealed on the map.
- **Rewards you keep.** Every boss now gives something lasting, shown on the map before it is won: the seven hair colours one by one, hearts, a bigger ink tank, a ring that makes forms last longer, bounties of pluses. Colours for bosses you have already beaten are yours when the game opens.
- **Call a colour.** A colour you own is called from the ribbon button by the arrows, for nine seconds, and refills after. Ribbons found in the lands still work.
- **Seven new bosses,** each with her own arena, entrance, three phases and attacks: the Guild Guard (land 6), Penchok-jipsa (12), Byeoru (35), Jupan (45), Sumbi (55), the Brake Pads (65) and the Straight-Ahead Party (75).
- Villagers and the stall's keeper no longer repeat what they said when she walks back to them.
- The stall is opened from a Shop sign over its keeper, and anyone with something for her has a mark over her head.

## The companion

- **Hair colour.** A colour won in Plus run can be worn by the animated Halgakos over your other apps: Settings, Halgakos, Hair colour. Colours not yet won are shown locked.

Page brawl is as it was in 3.0.2.
