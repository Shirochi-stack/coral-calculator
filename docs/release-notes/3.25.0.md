## Plus run: the final fight, made a finale

- **Lucy breathes ink fire** in the ride: a roaring stream of ink-black and indigo flame with violet and gold licks, poured from her jaws and following her head as she flies, with a flare at her mouth and an ink-fire explosion where it lands. It flies alongside Halgakos's beam.
- **Every beam comes from somewhere:** Halgakos's beam leaves the book she holds up and lands on the Mother Tongue with a splash of light. The Mother Tongue's beam gathers in her mouth before it swings across the sky.
- **When fire and beam hit her together,** light and ink fire burst on her, with rings and rays and a shaking screen.
- **The Mother Tongue's new area attacks:**
  - a wave of golden calligraphy across the sky
  - a ring of runes growing out from her
  - a rain of luminous pages with gaps to fly through
  - a beam that slants across the sky
  - turning ink sigils that burst into smaller ones
  - a desperation phase near the end, when she throws everything at once and the whole sky lights up from her
- **Her older attacks are redrawn:** walls and plates of rune light, and homing seals of violet and gold ink, in place of typed words on paper.
- **Fair warning:** every attack is marked in red where it will land before it arrives.
