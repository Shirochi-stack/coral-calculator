## Plus run, Volume Two

- **Fifty new lands** in five chapters, opened by clearing land 30: the Half-Title Palace, the Gazette Press, Between the Lines, the Slime Margins and the Appendix. Gokhae, the Gazette Twins, Yeobaek, King Slime and the Mawang wait at the ends of them. The map has a tab for each volume.
- **Lands with shape, from land 23 on.** Floating islands, climbs and drops, towers, a high road and a low one, low caves; ledges that crumble, belts, slime, draughts, moving ledges, bookmark doors, lava and water. Lands 23 to 30 were rebuilt this way too.
- **Villages and a shop inside the lands.** The webtoon's people stand in them and hand her something with a spoken word: Somi, Inju, Boksun-halmeoni, Ttosun, Galpi, Yeseo, Nabi, Moru, Queen Nuri. Altteul's stall sells bread, ink, ice cream and a ribbon for the pluses collected in that land.
- **Six new enemies,** each with a defeat of its own: slimes in ties, punctuation dragons, censor bars, Heart Guard fans, heavy words that fall, question marks that bound about. They come in crowds now, about thirty to a land where there were four to eight, and an ink gun is followed by a stretch full of them.
- **Eight ribbons** that change her hair and what she can do: red (fire, lava is safe), blue (water, she swims), brown (knocks over what she touches), green (flies), yellow (much faster), purple (sends back what is thrown), black (slow and harmless), and white, which is all of them but black.
- **Every boss is a real fight.** All eight, Illya, Tsubakieh and Zero included, now lock her in, make an entrance, and show a name and a health bar. Each has several attacks with a tell before them, can only be hurt in the moment after one, and changes twice as its health goes.
- **The story is told in the land.** A small box with the speaker's portrait takes the place of the full-screen cards. 218 spoken lines, 123 of them new, with new voices for the new speakers.
- **Fixes you asked for.** She walks under ledges and jumps up through them; the slash of the sword and brush is drawn where the blade is; a shut gate is a chained book, not a faint gate.

## Page brawl, fully animated

- **96 frames for every fighter:** standing, walking both ways, dashing, turning, the stages of a jump, guarding, three ways of being hit, falling, lying, getting up, winning and losing.
- **Chains.** Quick or heavy pressed again in time goes through five different blows, each drawn for itself; the two can be mixed. Blows landed in a run are counted on screen.
- **New moves:** three blows in the air, a jump-smash, a dash and a hop back, a guard-break, a launcher and a shove.
- **A dodge button.** For a moment nothing touches her; with an arrow held she slips through her opponent.
- **The ultimate has its own button,** which is not there until the meter is full and then bursts in.
- **Tap battle.** Two ultimates at once are settled by tapping: the faster tapper lands a greater ultimate.
- **Two players on one screen is removed.** Two devices over Wi-Fi remains; both need this version.

The app is larger now, about 31 MB, almost all of it the fighters' pictures and the voices.
