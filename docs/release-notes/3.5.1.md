## Page brawl: the old stance is back

- **Standing is the four drawn stance frames again,** as it was before 3.2.0: her weight shifting and her knees bending, in the same order and at the same pace, for all five fighters. The "breathing" that replaced it, first as six drawings that flickered and then as one drawing stretched in code, is gone.
- **Nothing resizes a fighter any more.** The stretch while standing, the squash a new frame came in with, the sway while dizzy and a small scale on the select screen are all removed.
- The wind-up and follow-through of blows, the fuller jump and the dodges are as they were in 3.3.2.

Plus run is as it was in 3.5.0.
