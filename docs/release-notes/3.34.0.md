## Plus run: Tulle & Thread, keyboard play, and sloppy copies

- **Tulle & Thread, the vanity boutique:** Tulle the seamstress sells outfits for Halgakos, fully voiced. Open it from the Vanity tab at the guild counter.
  - **Four new outfits drawn for every one of her animations:** Bunny Girl and Maid Dress, on sale, plus Sloppy☆Stage (her idol dress from the song) and the Seaside Swimsuit, each earned through one of Tulle's quests: fan letters, a lost bolt of silk, and a rare royal blue dye.
  - **The vanity choice** (Default, Show gear, Vanity) never changes what she can do.
- **Keyboard and mouse,** for Google Play Games on PC:
  - A and D or the arrow keys run.
  - W, Up or Space jumps.
  - S or Down goes down or through doors.
  - J or Enter attacks, K is the special, Q or Tab swaps, and E is the button over the jump.
  - Esc goes back.

  The on-screen buttons fade while you use keys, and Halgakos tells you the keys the first time you press one.
- **Sloppy copies:** the story now names what's spreading through the world of translation: pages copied by someone who never read them. Halgakos, Yeseo and Altteul have new voiced lines about them, and there's a new diary page.
- **Halgakos's own plus icon** now shows wherever pluses are counted or spent, and the guild counter has her shopping-cart picture.
- **Buildings, people and props stand on the ground,** with no gap under them, at every screen density.
