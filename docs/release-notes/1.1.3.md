## Wider buttons on the mini calculator

The two buttons at the top of the mini calculator, the arrow that opens the full calculator and the **✕** that closes it, are wider and now have the same rounded shape as the keys, so they are easier to see and to hit.

## Update

Download **Halgakos-Calculator-1.1.3.apk** below and open it on your Android device. Install over your current version to keep history and memory; no uninstall is needed.

Requires Android 8.0 or later. This is a debug-signed preview build.

Only the APK is needed to install. `SHA256SUMS.txt` is available for optional download verification. The code of Halgakos Calculator is licensed under [MIT](https://github.com/Shirochi-stack/coral-calculator/blob/master/LICENSE), with a copy included below; the Halgakos artwork is not.
