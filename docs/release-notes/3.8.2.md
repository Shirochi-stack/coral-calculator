## Page brawl: Chikuwa's brick bread drop

- **Brick bread drop has an animation of its own.** It used to play as a short rush and one overhead smash. Now she hugs a stone loaf as long as she is tall and heaves it over her head during the announcement, goes up high over the stage with it, and comes down on it as the move lands.
- **On contact** the fight stops for a moment with a white flash, loaves and rolls burst along the ground, the camera shakes, and there is a sound of its own: a falling whistle, a deep thud, crumbs.
- **After,** she is on top of the cracked loaf, then sitting on it eating a roll. The one who is hit is bounced off the ground, for the same damage as before.
- **Guarded,** it chips as before and pushes her well back.
- **Tsubakieh's greater home run,** the one a tap battle's winner uses, is now swung with her six home-run frames.

Plus run is as it was in 3.8.1.
