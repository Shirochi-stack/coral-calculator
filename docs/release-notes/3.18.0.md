## Plus run: Volume Three begins

- **Nineteen new lands** in two chapters, the Unread Shore (81 to 90) and the Dragon's Library (91 to 99), each with its own sky and ground. They are longer than any before. Land 100 is on the map, and shut for now.
- **A third page on the map.** With three volumes open the tabs read "Vol. 1", "Vol. 2" and "Vol. 3".
- **The friends pledge.** In each of the new lands one friend stands a little past the second flag. Walking up to her makes her pledge her help, with a card that counts how many are with Halgakos and a line of her own; a heart stays over her from then on. Nineteen in all, every one of them someone already met.
- **The story so far.** Volume Three falls broken, every page stopping halfway; the Mawang walks with her; Nuri names the Great Translation Killer; and Halgakos calls for every friend she has.
- **Twenty-eight new lines** in the voices already in the game.

These lands are the hardest yet: creatures take four blows, and a hurt costs two hearts, two and a half from land 91. Armour from the smithy is what they are for.

Page brawl is as it was in 3.8.3.
