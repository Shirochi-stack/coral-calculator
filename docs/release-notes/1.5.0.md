## More pranks, big and small

- **Four new whole-screen pranks**, twelve in all: she sticks notes all over the screen, throws confetti across it, draws the curtains on it, and takes a bite out of its corner.
- **Little pranks while she idles.** Between the big ones she plays small ones where she stands, a few seconds each: she turns upside down, twirls, shrinks, hops, dashes to the far side of the screen, or slips most of the way off its edge. A touch ends one. They come with **Surprise pranks**, which switches both kinds off.

## The giant Halgakos is sharp

When she grows to fill the screen she is now drawn from her full picture, not from her small frame blown up.

## "Where did my output go" only where it fits

She said it when she lost a game, was caught, took a bump, and in the cracked screen prank. It is now kept for what it means: a calculation in the mini calculator that ends with no answer.

## Download

Download **Halgakos-Calculator-1.5.0.apk** below and open it on your Android device. It installs over 1.2.0 to 1.4.1.

Coming from 1.1.4 or earlier? It installs beside your old one, your saved calculations do not carry over, and the old one should be uninstalled afterwards. See the [1.2.0 notes](https://github.com/Shirochi-stack/coral-calculator/releases/tag/v1.2.0).

Requires Android 8.0 or later. This is a debug-signed preview build.

Only the APK is needed to install. `SHA256SUMS.txt` is available for optional download verification. The code of Halgakos Calculator is licensed under [MIT](https://github.com/Shirochi-stack/coral-calculator/blob/master/LICENSE), with a copy included below; the Halgakos artwork is not.
