## Her head keeps its width while she idles

The animated Halgakos's hair was two or three pixels narrower in the frames where she blinks than in the frame she rests on. With her body held still since 1.3.2, that showed as her hair shrinking and growing back.

Her head is now one drawing in every idle frame, the same as her body and her tail: its outline is identical from frame to frame. It still bobs, and her eyes still close and open, each blink frame's own eyes set into that one head.

## Download

Download **Halgakos-Calculator-1.5.2.apk** below and open it on your Android device. It installs over 1.2.0 to 1.5.1.

Coming from 1.1.4 or earlier? It installs beside your old one, your saved calculations do not carry over, and the old one should be uninstalled afterwards. See the [1.2.0 notes](https://github.com/Shirochi-stack/coral-calculator/releases/tag/v1.2.0).

Requires Android 8.0 or later. This is a debug-signed preview build.
