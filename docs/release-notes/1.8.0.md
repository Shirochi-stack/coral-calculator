## Two more games, a hundred more things to say, and more pranks

**Play with Halgakos** now has twelve tiles.

### Plus run, over the whole screen

A run and jump across a land of signs, from one end to the gate at the other.

- Hold an arrow to run and the round button to jump. Hold it longer to jump higher. One finger can hold an arrow while another jumps.
- Collect the pluses. Land on the minuses that walk about; touch one any other way and it costs a heart.
- Mind the gaps. Falling into one costs a heart too.
- Three hearts. Two flags along the way are where you come back to.
- Reach the gate and your time is kept if it is your best.

### Last card, on the card

A game of getting rid of your cards, in a calculator's four signs.

- Seven cards each. Put down a card with the same sign or the same number as the last one.
- A **skip** or a **take two** costs Halgakos her turn, and she can do the same to you.
- An **any sign** card goes on anything, and you name the sign to follow.
- Nothing fits? Tap the pile to draw. If the card you draw fits you may put it down, or keep it.
- First to put down their last card wins.

### A hundred new lines

Halgakos has a hundred more things to say while idle, drawn from her character: her horns, wings, tail and cat ears, her translating and her glossary, her friends, her ice cream, her ego. Every one still ends on her catchphrase, or on the name you gave her to call you.

### More pranks

Four more over the whole screen, eighteen in all:

- She spills paint that runs down your screen.
- She makes it snow, until the snow lies deep along the bottom.
- She leaves the tap running, and the screen fills with water.
- She lets cobwebs grow in the corners, with a spider on a thread.

And three more small ones where she stands: she rocks from side to side, ducks flat, or jumps three times on the spot.

As ever, each prank is only a drawing over the screen, she owns up within a few seconds, and a tap ends it at once.

## Download

Download **Halgakos-Calculator-1.8.0.apk** below and open it on your Android device. It installs over 1.2.0 to 1.7.0.

Coming from 1.1.4 or earlier? It installs beside your old one, your saved calculations do not carry over, and the old one should be uninstalled afterwards. See the [1.2.0 notes](https://github.com/Shirochi-stack/coral-calculator/releases/tag/v1.2.0).

Requires Android 8.0 or later. This is a debug-signed preview build.
