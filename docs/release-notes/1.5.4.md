## Her face no longer flickers while she idles

1.5.2 and 1.5.3 held the animated Halgakos's head still and set each idle frame's own eyes into it. Her eyes, and the hair that falls across them, are drawn a little differently in every frame, so on a head that no longer moved they flickered: two patches of her face changed several times a second.

Now, while her eyes are open, every idle frame has exactly the same face. Only the three frames of her blink differ, and in those only her eyes change, not the hair around them. Her tail still swings.
