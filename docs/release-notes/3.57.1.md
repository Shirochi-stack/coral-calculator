## Plus run: see-through buttons

- **The weapon and swap buttons have no disc behind them any more:** just the weapon, on a clear background with a thin ring of light and a soft shade, so it reads over sky and cave alike. Pressed, they fill with coral.
- **The arrows and the jump are glass rings** with white arrowheads instead of white discs, on foot, on the ride and in flight.
- **The down button is easier to see** over bright ground, and still fades when she stands over it.
