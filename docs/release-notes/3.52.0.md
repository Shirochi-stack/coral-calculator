## Plus run: Knuckles, and the skill tree redrawn

- **Knuckles, a new weapon class:** tap for jab, jab, uppercut (the uppercut leaves creatures reeling); tap while running for a dash punch; hold for a haymaker that breaks any shield in front of her outright. Knuckles come from every chapter with their own icons, plus paper knuckles to fold at the smithy and a Halgakos Rose Knuckles. Brawlers have their own armour and accessory, the Brawler's Gi and Champion's Wraps, from bosses. The Mother Tongue's Quill now needs all 13 Roses; a save that had the 12 is given the knuckle Rose at the smithy.
- **The skill tree is redrawn as a web:** 13 class regions around a shared core, with clusters of skills round a notable, dead-end branches out to deep notables and keystones, routes between neighbouring classes for hybrid builds, and no nodes overlapping.
- **148 notables, each with its own icon,** that change how weapons play: shots that fan, pierce, jump to the next creature or bounce off walls; swings that strike twice or send thrown things back; shockwaves; slowing ink pools; combo meters; life and energy back on a kill; and keystones such as Blood Ink and Iron Reflexes.
- **No more dull trade-offs:** plain stat skills have no downside. Only keystones and a few deep notables have a cost.
- **Your points are back:** the first time you open the game, every point your level has earned is free to spend again, with a note on the tree and a word from Halgakos.
- **Outfits cover more:** every vanity outfit and ear and horn style now covers climbing and knuckles, and the Pajama Set, Gothic Dress and Knight's Tabard are drawn whole on every animation.
- **A fix:** some big found weapons (knuckles, greatsword, mace, hammer, scythe) were stripping shields far too fast during their swing.
