## Games, one tap away

- **A games button on the calculator.** A game pad beside Halgakos, at the top of the calculator, opens Play with Halgakos.
- **Games first.** A new setting under Play with Halgakos: the tap on the floating Halgakos that opens the mini calculator opens her games in its place, two taps where two are asked for. With it on she says "Tap me twice to play with me" where she said "for the calculator". The mini calculator is still in her menu.
- **Open the app on the games.** Another new setting: opened from its icon, the app starts on Play with Halgakos, with the calculator one step back.
- The Plus run banner counts all eighty lands.

Plus run and Page brawl are as they were in 3.5.1.

## Download

Download **Halgakos-Calculator-3.5.2.apk** below and open it on your Android device. It installs over 1.2.0 to 3.5.1.

Requires Android 8.0 or later. This is a debug-signed preview build.

Only the APK is needed to install. `SHA256SUMS.txt` is available for optional download verification. The code of Halgakos Calculator is licensed under [MIT](https://github.com/Shirochi-stack/coral-calculator/blob/master/LICENSE), with a copy included below; the Halgakos artwork is not.
