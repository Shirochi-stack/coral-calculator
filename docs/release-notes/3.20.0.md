## Plus run: the Mother Tongue, and the true ending

- **The story's last turn.** When the flight is won and Lucy's book is read as it was written, the one who had it changed answers from the sky.
- **A fourth part to land 100.** Halgakos rides on Lucy's back, in a sky of pages, against the Mother Tongue. The arrows steer, holding strike gathers the white beam, and a new button is Lucy's breath.
- **Her shield comes off in pieces.** Lucy's breath takes one, and so, every few seconds, does one of the friends who pledged. Only with the shield gone does the beam read her, and the last reading needs beam and breath together.
- **What she sends:** rows of "Mr.", "Miss", "Sir" and "Madam" with one way through, a press that closes from above and below, names that follow until they are cut, and her own pass down one side of the sky. Each shows where it will land.
- **The true ending** is kept once the ride is won. Whoever won land 100 before plays it again to reach it.
- **The Mother Tongue has a voice of her own,** and fourteen new lines are spoken between her, Lucy and Halgakos.
- **The four lines that closed land 100** are now told before the reveal, where they lead into it.

Page brawl is as it was in 3.8.3.
