## Plus run: white attacks

- **White throws what its colours throw.** Red and blue worn together throw one cloud of steam in place of fire and water, and white, being every colour, throws that steam. The steam flew and beat what it met, but it was never drawn, so white, and plain red with blue, looked as if they threw nothing. It is drawn now: a rolling white cloud with the flame and the drop in it.
- **White has every colour's power,** each now tested by name: steam with every strike, whatever she carries; lava and water hold her up; brown's touch and half hurt from what is thrown; purple sends back what is thrown; green's flight; yellow's speed.
- **The attack button shows what a strike will do:** what she carries, with the fire, the water or both on its corner, and a rim of white and gold in white.
- White worn from her colours for a while is still the unmastered one: a time on it, a faint glow, and no beam.

The true white, with its beam through walls and ledges, its speed and its full halo, is built and waits for the story to give it in the last volume.

Page brawl is as it was in 3.6.2.
