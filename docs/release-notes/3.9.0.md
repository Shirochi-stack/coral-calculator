## Plus run: smaller creatures, more of them

- **Creatures are four fifths the size.** Walkers, hoppers, throwers, shooters and fliers are drawn a little smaller, and are touched exactly where they are drawn. Bosses, roaming bosses, outsized creatures and cellar giants keep their size.
- **The creatures added to lands were too big, and no longer are.** Since 3.7.0 every creature added to make up a land's numbers was being drawn and touched at the outsized size, a third bigger than the land's own. Against what has been played since then, a pack member is now well under two thirds the size it was.
- **Lands hold over four times the creatures they first had,** up from about three, with room still kept to come down among a pack. Twenty-one lands are at five times; the rest are as full as their free ground allows, the short boss lands least. Lands 1 to 3 stay at twice.
- **Fair to hit.** A stomp is as forgiving on a small creature as it was on the old size, with a hair to either side when she is coming down on it, and what she throws and swings reaches a small one from as far as before.
- **Crowds wake later.** Added creatures keep still until she is within sixteen squares, not twenty-six, so several packs no longer come at her at once.
- **Cellars** keep their numbers, with thirty-two in the room at once where there were twenty-four.

Page brawl is as it was in 3.8.3.
