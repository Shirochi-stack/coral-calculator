## Plus run: the smithy, and three sisters

- **A smithy in the villages.** A fourth sort of building, with Moru at its anvil. From land 13 her two sisters work there too: **Pulmu**, who makes armour, loud and sunny in a red bandana, and **Sutdol**, who trades in metal and re-forges, calm and exact behind a brass loupe. All three carry double-headed hammers.
- **Weapons are forged in three steps,** paid in pluses and what the hunts pay. Each step changes what the weapon does, not only how hard it hits:
  - Sword: a slash wave ahead; then further and through everything; then two waves, taller.
  - Knife: a thin wave with each throw; then three in a fan; then further and through.
  - Brush: a wide wave of paint; then further, wiping away what is thrown at her; then further and taller.
  - Pen: goes through the first thing; then bursts where it ends; then through everything, with a wider burst.
  - Book: a wider burst; then its pages burn where it burst; then wider and longer.
  - Ink gun: its blots splash; then a blot jumps on to the next creature; then wider, two jumps.
  - Tablet-book: two pages; then a wider, taller panel; then a page bursts where it turns back.
  - API keys: seven in the fan; then keys go through; then the master key, a piece more off a shield.
- **Armour,** made by Pulmu: a helm, a coat, boots and a charm, each in three sets. The helm takes half a heart off a hurt, the coat keeps her safe for longer after one, the boots make landing on a creature count for more, the charm keeps more pluses at a land's end, and all four of one set give a heart more. It is put on at the smithy or from Gear on the map, and shows as small pictures under her hearts.
- **Trade,** with Sutdol: type metal, ink crystal and gold leaf bought and sold for pluses, and a weapon re-forged back to its first level with all its metal returned.
- **Lands get tougher.** Every land has a level, one more every five lands. Creatures take one more blow from land 26, two from 51 and three from 76; a hurt costs a heart and a half from land 31 and two hearts from land 61. Bosses and the roaming bosses are as they were. The map shows each chapter's levels, a card says so the first time a land is tougher than any before, and marks over a creature show the blows it still takes.
- **Villages have changed.** A village still has two buildings to go into, now chosen from four sorts, so which stand in which land is not as it was in 3.14.0.
- **Six new lines,** two for each sister.

Page brawl is as it was in 3.8.3.
