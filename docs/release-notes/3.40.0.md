## Plus run: the skill tree

- **A skill tree with 1,465 skills:** 24 paths branching from the centre, two for each weapon class. Open it from the round seed button beside Gear on the map, or Skills on the pause card. A dot shows when points are waiting.
  - **Small skills:** +1 HP, defence, intelligence or attack.
  - **Big roots:** +3, +5, +10 or +20.
  - **12 notable skills on every path:** passives, strong passives that come with a drawback, actives, and a keystone at the end.
  - **The paths link together** near their ends.
- **Points with no limit:** one for every level, plus a bonus every tenth level. Existing saves get all the points their level has earned, ready to spend.
- **Moving around the tree:** drag to look around, and zoom with a pinch, the + and − buttons or the mouse wheel. Tap a skill to see what it does and take it.
- **Stats page and respec:** a stats page shows HP, defence, intelligence, attack, energy and divine power. The first respec is free; after that it costs pluses.
- **Active skills:** up to three on the screen beside the swap button, used by touch or with keys 1, 2 and 3. Each costs energy, lasts 8 seconds and then rests.
- **Divine power:** a new bar beside energy. When it fills, her blows count three times for 6 seconds.
- **Hearts are worth 20 HP each.** Past 20 hearts, they change colour in layers instead of filling the screen.
- **Halgakos has three new voiced lines** for points to spend, divine power and respec.
