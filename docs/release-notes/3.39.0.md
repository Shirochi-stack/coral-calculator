## Plus run: the films, closer to right

- **The prologue has its sound effects back** in English, Korean and Chinese: the original film's hits now play under Halgakos and the music.
- **The idol song is re-timed to the singers' mouths**, in all four languages and in both the upright and wide films, with shortened lines and the music kept underneath. Halgakos is heard wherever her own mouth moves in her close-ups. This is an interim version: the group shots and a few overlapping lines are still being fixed.
- **Captions wrap** to up to three lines and shrink to fit, so a long line never runs off the screen, in any language.
