## Plus run: ultimates, a vampire merchant, and potions

- **Six ultimates** at the ends of the skill tree, each changing how she fights for a while, in soft anime colours: the Rampage of the Red Page (greatswords: rose flames, twice the blows, every blow heals, but no guard), the Stilled Hour (katana: the world slows to a crawl and her blows land all at once), the Tide of Ink (pens: she becomes a wave through everything), the Colossus of Contents (hammers: two and a half times her size), the Legion of Echoes (staffs: three echoes strike with her), and Rose Bloom (scythes: untouchable, and every fall blooms into the next).
- **The Vampire Merchant** keeps a stall in the dungeons: gifts she likes and does not, prices that fall the fonder she grows, three warnings if she is struck, and a fight that cannot be won until much later. She speaks every line.
- **Potions**: a pouch button on the left, eighteen bottles each with its own shape, a drink that must be finished before it works (and spilled by a hit), heals that share a cooldown, buffs on their own timers, and rare permanent ones from her shelf.
- **Alchemy in the skill tree**: Quick Sip, Steady Hands, Herbalist and Second Draught.
