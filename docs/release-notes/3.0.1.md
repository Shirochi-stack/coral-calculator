## Voices

- **Rune has a new voice,** designed for her as a timid crybaby and picked from twenty-two samples. Her sniffs are now acted, not read out as the word.
- **Chikuwa** says "bread" properly. Her voice is otherwise as it was.

In Plus run and in Page brawl. Nothing else is changed from 3.0.0.

## Download

Download **Halgakos-Calculator-3.0.1.apk** below and open it on your Android device. It installs over 1.2.0 to 3.0.0.

Requires Android 8.0 or later. This is a debug-signed preview build.

Only the APK is needed to install. `SHA256SUMS.txt` is available for optional download verification. The code of Halgakos Calculator is licensed under [MIT](https://github.com/Shirochi-stack/coral-calculator/blob/master/LICENSE), with a copy included below; the Halgakos artwork is not.
