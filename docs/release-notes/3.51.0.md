## Plus run: real places, climbing, and bosses that come for you

- **Underwater, for real:** lagoons now have a sandy seabed with coral and weed, sea-rock walls and pillars, and light rays from the surface. Water everywhere is drawn as deep water, pale at the top and dark blue below, with no tiles showing.
- **Dungeons get darker as you go down:** floors 1 to 5 keep their dungeon's look; floors 6 to 10 turn grittier (a deep catacomb, ink crypt, paper cave or vault, on cracked stone with roots); from floor 11, the abyss, on black rock with glowing cracks. Each depth has its own light.
- **Hideouts feel like a basement:** brick arches, shelves of jars and books, pipes, candles and drifting dust, with a proper stone floor. The cellars of waves have the same floor and brick.
- **The map of lands in landscape:** on a phone on its side the chapters sit side by side with bigger lands, and on a tablet in two columns. Portrait is unchanged.
- **Halgakos climbs:** ladders and vines get a hand-over-hand climb. Until the outfits are drawn for it, she climbs in her outfit using her jump frames.
- **Shields are a real obstacle:** from land 6 a shield needs a strong weapon, a forged one, or coming down on it from above. A plain sword no longer breaks it. Bosses' shields have one more piece.
- **No hiding from bosses:** every boss now leaps up onto her ledge after a moment, with a glowing crouch first and a slam when it lands, or shoots up at her if it can't fit. Standing on a platform is no longer safe.
