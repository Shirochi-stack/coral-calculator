## Four more pranks, with her wave and her look

The animated Halgakos now has two more of her animations in the app: her hand wave, and her look in sixteen directions. Four new pranks use them.

Over the whole screen, from **Prank me** or by herself while idle:

- **Her sisters.** A row of her comes up along the bottom of the screen, every one waving. "Say hi to my sisters Sloppy~♡"
- **The bug.** She draws a ladybird that crawls across your screen, and her eyes follow it wherever it goes. "Eek! There's a bug on your screen Sloppy~♡"

Where she stands, among her small idle pranks:

- **A wave.** "Hiii~ Did you forget about me Sloppy~♡"
- **A look round.** She looks all the way round her, twice. "Hm? What's that behind you Sloppy~♡"

As with every prank, each is only a drawing over the screen, she owns up within a few seconds, and a tap ends it at once. The two small ones play only for the animated Halgakos; the still one keeps to the others.

## Fixes

- The green screen left in her hand wave, a yellow and green streak beside her hip, is cut out.
- Her pinks are matched across animations more closely than before.
- A touch during one of her small pranks now always ends the prank and does nothing else. Before, it could also open the mini calculator or her menu.

## Download

Download **Halgakos-Calculator-1.6.0.apk** below and open it on your Android device. It installs over 1.2.0 to 1.5.4.

Coming from 1.1.4 or earlier? It installs beside your old one, your saved calculations do not carry over, and the old one should be uninstalled afterwards. See the [1.2.0 notes](https://github.com/Shirochi-stack/coral-calculator/releases/tag/v1.2.0).

Requires Android 8.0 or later. This is a debug-signed preview build.
