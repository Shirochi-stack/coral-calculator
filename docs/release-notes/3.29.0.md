## Plus run: levels with no cap, and loot

- **Halgakos levels up, with no cap.** Every creature and boss gives experience. Creatures in lands far below her level give only a little, so going back to older lands helps without making them pointless.
  - Each level makes her blows stronger. Every so often a level also makes hits cost her less or adds a heart.
  - Her level and a gold experience bar sit under the top-left plate in every land, and her level shows on the map.
  - When she levels up the plate swells with sparks and she says so. There's no pop-up.
  - Existing saves start at the level their won lands would have given.
- **Each chapter's "levels X to Y" on the map** now shows the level its lands are meant for.
- **Pluses drop like coins:** every creature drops pluses that burst out, bounce and come to rest on the ground. They're drawn to her when she's close and blink before they vanish.
- **Rare loot:** now and then a creature drops a weapon, armour or accessory themed to its chapter, and bosses always drop one. It sits under a beam in its rarity's colour: White, Blue, Purple, Gold, Red, or Halgakos Rose, whose beam shifts colour. Walk into it to put it in her bag.
- Equipping this loot and its art arrive in coming updates.
