## Plus run: the first animated scene

- **The Mother Tongue comes down in a clip of animation.** In land 100, after the flight is won, the telling reaches the moment Halgakos names the great evil, and a six-second animated scene plays there: the sky of pages tears, the Mother Tongue descends, and Halgakos says her line, "The great evil. Not translation. You Sloppy~♡".
- **A tap skips it,** and so does Back. The words are shown under the picture.
- **It keeps to the game's sound setting,** and plays silent when it is off.
- **If the clip cannot play** on a phone, the line is told as it was before.
- **Leaving the app while it plays** puts the game away as usual; coming back carries on with the next line.

The scene adds about 1.7 MB to the app.

Page brawl is as it was in 3.8.3.
