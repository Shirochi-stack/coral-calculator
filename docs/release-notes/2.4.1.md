## Voice touch-ups

- **Rune** is lower and softer: tearful, not squeaky.
- **Chikuwa** is bouncier and more sing-song, like the cheerful one of an anime cast.
- **Illya** squeals a Japanese "kyaa" where she squeals.

In Plus run and in Page brawl. Nothing else is changed from 2.4.0.

## Download

Download **Halgakos-Calculator-2.4.1.apk** below and open it on your Android device. It installs over 1.2.0 to 2.4.0.

Requires Android 8.0 or later. This is a debug-signed preview build.
