## Two more games: chess and minesweeper

**Play with Halgakos** now has ten tiles.

### Chess, on the card

You have white and move first; Halgakos has black. Tap a piece and the squares it may go to are marked: a dot on an empty square, a ring round a piece it would take. Tap one to move there. The last move is lit, and so is a king in check.

Every rule is played: castling, capturing in passing, promotion (a pawn that reaches the last row becomes a queen), checkmate, stalemate, and the draws by repetition, by fifty quiet moves and by too little left to mate with. She looks three moves ahead, follows exchanges through to the end, and does not always answer the same way. Wins, losses and draws are kept.

### Minesweeper, over the whole screen

A minefield as large as your screen allows, with about one mine to every six or seven squares. On a phone that is around 120 squares and 19 mines.

- Tap a square to open it. Its number is how many mines touch it.
- Hold a square to flag a mine, or switch on the flag button at the top to flag with a tap.
- The first square you open is never a mine, and always opens some room.
- Tap an open number that already has all its flags, and the rest of the squares round it open.

Strike a mine and every mine shows, with the one you struck in red and any wrong flag crossed through. Clear the field and your time is kept if it is your best.

## The menu

With ten games, each tile now has its picture beside its name instead of above it.

## Download

Download **Halgakos-Calculator-1.7.0.apk** below and open it on your Android device. It installs over 1.2.0 to 1.6.0.

Coming from 1.1.4 or earlier? It installs beside your old one, your saved calculations do not carry over, and the old one should be uninstalled afterwards. See the [1.2.0 notes](https://github.com/Shirochi-stack/coral-calculator/releases/tag/v1.2.0).

Requires Android 8.0 or later. This is a debug-signed preview build.
