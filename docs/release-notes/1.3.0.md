## Play with Halgakos

Halgakos now plays games, plays pranks, and talks.

**Hold her and choose Play with Halgakos** for a card of games where the mini calculator opens. The arrow in its corner carries the game on to a full screen in the app, which also opens from **Settings › Play with Halgakos**.

On the card:

- **Tic-tac-toe**, with you as the crosses. She plays well, but she can be beaten.
- **Rock paper scissors**, first to three.
- **Pairs**: sixteen cards, eight pairs of a calculator's signs.
- **Quick sums**: thirty seconds of small calculations.

All over the screen, on top of whatever app you are in:

- **Catch Halgakos**: she runs about the whole screen, faster each time you catch her.
- **Number rain**: drag to move her, catch each **+**, keep clear of each **−**.
- **Bubble count**: pop ten numbered bubbles from the smallest to the largest.

The **✕** in the corner, or Back, leaves one of these.

## Pranks

**Prank me**, among her games, has her make off with the calculator's icon and bin it, crack the screen, or scribble on it. It is only a drawing on top of the screen: nothing is deleted or changed, she owns up within a few seconds, and a tap brings that on at once. **Surprise pranks**, in her settings and off by default, lets her play one now and then without being asked.

## She talks

- A speech bubble pops up over her head now and then while she is idle. **Speech bubbles** in her settings turns them off, and a tap sends one away.
- Her catchphrase, **"Where did my output go?!"**, comes out whenever something of hers goes wrong: a game she loses, a bump in Number rain, being caught, and a calculation in the mini calculator that ends in an error.

## Foldables

- On an open foldable, a game's cards and Number rain keep to her side of the fold.
- On the full screen in the app she takes one side of the fold and the games the other.
- Folding or turning the device in the middle of a game refits it to the new screen.

## Download

Download **Halgakos-Calculator-1.3.0.apk** below and open it on your Android device. It installs over 1.2.0 to 1.2.6.

Coming from 1.1.4 or earlier? It installs beside your old one, your saved calculations do not carry over, and the old one should be uninstalled afterwards. See the [1.2.0 notes](https://github.com/Shirochi-stack/coral-calculator/releases/tag/v1.2.0).

Requires Android 8.0 or later. This is a debug-signed preview build.
