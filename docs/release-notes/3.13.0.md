## Plus run: villages with buildings to go into

- **Two doors in every village.** Each village now has two buildings she can walk into, of different sorts chosen by the land and by which of its villages it is, so villages no longer look the same: a pet shop, the guild hall, or a home.
- **The pet shop.** The ten pets sit on cushions along the floor and on a shelf, each with its price, or "hers" once she owns it. Walking up to one gives a Look button that opens the counter's Pets tab on that pet. Nabi keeps the shop.
- **The guild hall.** Inju stands at the desk, and the button there opens the guild counter. Its hunts board is still to come.
- **A home.** Someone is in, with a treat, and a few pluses lie on the floor.
- **Safe inside.** Nothing in a building hurts her. The land outside is laid exactly as it was.
- **Tabs of one size.** The counter's six tabs share their width by the length of their words.
- **Pops stay on the screen.** The word over a pet there is no room for used to run off the left edge.

Still to come: the smithy, the black market, and the guild hall's hunts.

Page brawl is as it was in 3.8.3.
