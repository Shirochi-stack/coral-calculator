## Plus run: real gear

- **Every item has its own drawn icon:** 228 of them, covering weapons for each chapter, Halgakos Rose weapons, armour and accessories. The same icon shows on the ground under its beam, on the pickup card and in the bag.
- **Drops stay on the ground:** loot and pluses no longer sink into steps or the ground. They come to rest on top of whatever is under them, even at a low frame rate.
- **Accessories are rare and strong.** Ordinary creatures seldom drop one and never above Blue, while bosses always drop Purple or better. Each accessory suits a class of weapon (blades, tomes, throwing, heavy, ink guns, wands, reapers) or any weapon. Higher rarities give bigger numbers and extra effects.
- **Armour** gives a chance to shrug off hits and harder blows for the class it suits. Armour and accessories from the same chapter form sets with bonuses.
- **New on the screen:**
  - an energy bar for specials
  - a shield bar above the hearts that refills after a few seconds
  - life regen
  - dodges, thorns and "Great blow!" hits
  - poison, burn and freeze on creatures
- **Wear your gear now:** "Bag ›" in the Backpack, from Pause or from the map's Gear, has slots for armour and two accessories. Changes take effect at once. Weapons you find stay in the bag until the next update.
