## Plus run: a backpack, and weapons of her own

- **A backpack.** A weapon found once is hers for good, in every land. It goes into the backpack and into her hands, with a banner that says so. A weapon she already has, lying in a land, gives something else: the ink gun 8 more shots, the book 2 more books, any other 15 seconds of what she holds being stronger.
- **Switching.** A button over the strike button: a tap goes between the last two she used, and holding it opens the backpack while the land waits. The backpack is also on the pause card, and on the map as "Gear".
- **A card for every weapon.** Its power, reach, blows a second, what it takes off a shield, what it holds and has left, what it does, and what it is good and bad at. The figures are the ones the game itself uses.
- **Ammunition is each weapon's own.** The ink gun begins a land with its tank and can hold twice that; books are six to a land. Empty, a weapon is put away and she holds her main weapon.
- **The tablet-book, her main weapon.** Hers from the start, and she begins every land holding it. A tap flicks a page five squares out and back, through everything both ways. Held, a panel of translation opens in front of her: it takes everything standing in it, wipes away what is thrown into it, and takes two pieces off a shield.
- **The API keys, a second main weapon.** A ring of keys lies in one land in five from land 7; found once it is hers, and "Gear" on the map chooses which main weapon she begins with. A tap throws a key: what it sticks in is unlocked for four seconds, and the next blow on it that is not a key counts twice. Held, the whole ring goes out in a fan of five. Now and then a key fails with a "401" over her head, never twice in twenty seconds and never while a boss is open to be hurt.
- **Drawn for her.** Twelve frames for each of the two, drawn together, dyed with her hair like the rest.

Still to come: the smith's levels, the katana and the other weapons of the black arena.

Page brawl is as it was in 3.8.3.
