## Halgakos: The Queen of Sloppies

- **A new name:** the game is now **Halgakos: The Queen of Sloppies**. On your home screen it is still just Halgakos.

## Plus run

- **One inventory for everything:** the backpack and the bag are now a single screen. Five gear slots sit round Halgakos in her current look: main weapon, secondary weapon (the swap button switches to it), armour and two accessories, with outlines where a slot is empty. Below, every weapon, armour and accessory in one grid, with tabs, and sorting by rarity, weapon type or newest. Tap one for its stats, a comparison with what she has on, and Equip as main or secondary. It lays itself out differently in portrait and landscape, and works with a keyboard.
- **Twelve new creatures** for chapters 6 to 11, two per chapter, each fighting its own way and tougher than the old ones, each with a word from Halgakos the first time she meets it.
- **Secrets are secret now:** a hidden alcove or false wall looks like plain rock until it is found: nothing of what is inside shows through. A faint crack may glint on one stone when she is close. Strike it to break it open, or walk into a false wall.
- **Pets climb too:** running pets climb the ladder or vine after her, hand over hand.
- **The skill tree's regions each have their own icons:** every class's nodes show its weapon in its colour.
- **Prank me is easier to follow:** the scenes play slower, so you can read what she says and react.
