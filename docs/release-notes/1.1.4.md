## Mini calculator buttons without a background

The arrow and the **✕** at the top of the mini calculator are bare icons again, as they were before 1.1.3. They keep the wider touch area that release gave them, and still show a rounded highlight while pressed.

## Update

Download **Halgakos-Calculator-1.1.4.apk** below and open it on your Android device. Install over your current version to keep history and memory; no uninstall is needed.

Requires Android 8.0 or later. This is a debug-signed preview build.

Only the APK is needed to install. `SHA256SUMS.txt` is available for optional download verification. The code of Halgakos Calculator is licensed under [MIT](https://github.com/Shirochi-stack/coral-calculator/blob/master/LICENSE), with a copy included below; the Halgakos artwork is not.
