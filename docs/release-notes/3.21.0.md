## Plus run: a harder last volume, and the true white to call

- **Volume Three is harder.** In lands 81 to 90 creatures take five blows where they took four, and a hurt costs two hearts and a half where it cost two. In lands 91 to 100 creatures take five blows, and a hurt costs three hearts where it cost two and a half. Lands 1 to 80 are as they were, and the black market's Harder steps still add on top.
- **Land 100's fights are harder too.** Lucy's shield has a piece more in each part and she gives a little less warning; in the flight what she sends comes sooner; the Mother Tongue's shield has a piece more in each stage. A hurt in the flight and on the ride costs what the land's does. Every attack still shows where it will land.
- **The true white is hers to call.** Once land 100 is won with the ribbon turned, "White, and hers" is the first choice in the colour picker, in gold. One tap calls it for the rest of the land: every colour at once, the light, both wings, and the white beam on a held strike, which rests two and a half seconds between uses. It cannot be mixed, cannot be called under black, and cannot be called in land 100, where the ribbon still has to turn.
- **The card at the start of a tougher land** now says what a hurt truly costs there. It stopped at "two hearts" before, even where the cost was more.

With three hearts and nothing worn, one hurt in lands 91 to 100 is a life. Armour from the smithy and hearts from the counter are what those lands are for.

Page brawl is as it was in 3.8.3.
