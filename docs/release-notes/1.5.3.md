## She stands still while she idles

In 1.5.2 the animated Halgakos's head kept its width but still rode up and down by two or three pixels over a body that held still, which looked like her head sliding on her shoulders.

She now holds still from head to feet while she idles: every idle frame is one drawing. Two things move, her eyes as she blinks and her tail as it swings.

## Download

Download **Halgakos-Calculator-1.5.3.apk** below and open it on your Android device. It installs over 1.2.0 to 1.5.2.

Coming from 1.1.4 or earlier? It installs beside your old one, your saved calculations do not carry over, and the old one should be uninstalled afterwards. See the [1.2.0 notes](https://github.com/Shirochi-stack/coral-calculator/releases/tag/v1.2.0).

Requires Android 8.0 or later. This is a debug-signed preview build.
