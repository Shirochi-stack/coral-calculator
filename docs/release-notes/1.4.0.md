## More pranks, on more than the calculator

- **Her delete prank takes any app.** She carries off one of the apps in your launcher, by its own icon and name, and bins it: "Oops~ I deleted Calendar Sloppy~♡". It is still only a drawing; the app is where it was.
- **Five new pranks.** She shakes the screen and your apps fall in a heap. She turns the screen off. She drops her ice cream on it. She walks all over it and leaves paw prints. She grows until she fills it.
- **No repeats until she has been through them all**, eight in all with the cracked screen and the scribble.

## Speech bubbles that behave

- **A bubble no longer vanishes when you touch her.** Tap her, hold her or drag her and she keeps talking; the bubble follows her. A tap on the bubble itself still sends it away.
- **A bubble never lies over her, her menu or the mini calculator.** It points at her with a little tail from above or below, and moves over the top of the menu or the calculator when one opens.

## Her catchphrase

- **Every line of hers ends on it now**, in the games and the pranks as well, and there are over 170 of them, up from 108.
- **What she calls you** is a new field in her settings. Leave it empty and you are Sloppy; type another name and her catchphrase is that name instead: "Play with me Boss~♡".

## Her apology stays her size

The animated Halgakos was drawn larger while she bowed, her head up to a third wider. Those frames are brought down to the size she is in her other animations.

## Privacy

Her pranks now read the names and icons of the apps in your launcher, to draw them. They are not kept or sent anywhere, and the app still has no permission to use the network. The [privacy policy](https://github.com/Shirochi-stack/coral-calculator/blob/master/PRIVACY.md) says so.

## Download

Download **Halgakos-Calculator-1.4.0.apk** below and open it on your Android device. It installs over 1.2.0 to 1.3.2.

Coming from 1.1.4 or earlier? It installs beside your old one, your saved calculations do not carry over, and the old one should be uninstalled afterwards. See the [1.2.0 notes](https://github.com/Shirochi-stack/coral-calculator/releases/tag/v1.2.0).

Requires Android 8.0 or later. This is a debug-signed preview build.
