## Plus run: passable crowds, an opening chest

- **Crowds you can get through.** The added creatures no longer stand shoulder to shoulder: a pack's members are a square apart, a spiked times sign comes alone, the first twelve lands have them in ones and twos, and none stand near a roaming boss's place or the start of a land. With room kept to land among them, the lands hold about three times the creatures they first had, not five; the fliers are three times.
- **More to fight with:** another weapon every forty squares or so of a land.
- **The first two bosses are gentler.** The Guild Guard and Penchok-jipsa keep a two-piece shield to the end, with no throw-back.
- **The chest opens.** It jumps, its lid flies off, light comes out, and the pluses go up in arcs to the count at the top. A piece of a heart flies to her hearts.
- **Thin ledges are marked.** A small arrowhead under them shows they can be dropped through, brighter under the one she stands on and with the lights out.
- A streak of landings pays four at most, as shots do.
- The bubble and the card keep clear of the pet's plate.

Page brawl is as it was in 3.6.2.
