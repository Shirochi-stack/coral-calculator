## Page brawl

- **Dodges of their own.** Dodging away, dodging toward and dodging in the air each have their own animation for every fighter: Halgakos springs back through a page, Chikuwa hops behind her basket, Tsubakieh takes one long step with the bat on her shoulder, Rune slides back as ink, and Illya twirls away in sparkles.
- **The ultimate clash starts when it should.** Answer an ultimate with your own before any of it has landed and the tap battle begins, not only while it is being announced.
- A button tapped at the instant a blow lands is no longer dropped.
- Fighters no longer slide off the edge of the screen during a fast dodge.

Nothing else is changed from 3.0.1.

## Download

Download **Halgakos-Calculator-3.0.2.apk** below and open it on your Android device. It installs over 1.2.0 to 3.0.1.

Requires Android 8.0 or later. This is a debug-signed preview build.
