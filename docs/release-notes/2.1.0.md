## Plus run tells the webtoon's story now

The story of Plus run is *A Book Fell from the Sky*: the World's Largest Book lands on Halgakos on her day off, she wakes inside it, and Illya means to rewrite the Last Page with herself as its only heroine. It is told on cards by whoever is speaking, with her own picture and her own voice.

- **The cast is in the game.** Chikuwa waits by the first flag with bread (a heart back) from land 3; Rune waits by the second with a ball of her ink (it takes one hurt for you) from land 9. Illya waits at the end of land 18, Tsubakieh at the end of land 24, and Zero, the "none" of Illya's "Supporting cast: none", on the Last Page. The Mawang speaks too, and leaves her notes before Zero.
- **Illya** floats, keeps her distance and fires hearts that turn the arrows the wrong way round for a moment. **Tsubakieh** walks up, swings Normal No. 2, and bats back whatever is thrown at her. Each has a flag at her door.
- **Every line re-recorded, 95 of them.** Each is now said about what is actually happening: the first time each sign, spike, spring or flag comes into view, what it was that hurt her, how many stars a land ended on. The voice service is told the scene of every line with its reasoning switched on, so a yelp sounds like a yelp and a sulk like a sulk. Chikuwa, Rune, Illya and Tsubakieh have anime voices of their own; the Mawang has Halgakos's, slower and lower.

## An in-game shop, and things that help

- **Guild counter.** The pluses you collect are yours to spend, whether or not you reach the gate. **Guild counter: upgrades**, on the map, sells what she keeps in every land: a fourth and fifth heart, her wings (a second jump in the air), a magnet for pluses, longer-lasting treats and a bigger ink tank.
- **Treats in the lands.** Ice cream makes her faster and her jump higher; a magnet brings pluses to her; a page of notes makes her untouchable for a few seconds. What is helping shows under her hearts with how much of it is left.
- **Combos.** Land on one sign after another without touching the ground and each is worth one plus more than the last.

## Her, carrying things

With a pen, a knife, a book, a brush, a sword or the ink gun in her hands she now has frames of her own for standing, running, jumping and using it, drawn for each of the six and matched to her other frames for colour and size.

## Controls

The four round buttons are redrawn, with a soft shadow, a rim of light and rounded arrowheads; they sink a little when held, and the two arrows stand further apart.
