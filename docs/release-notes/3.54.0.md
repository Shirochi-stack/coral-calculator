## Plus run: burning, freezing, poison and stun, flashier effects, twelve new creatures

- **Status effects:**
  - **Burn** spreads from one creature to the next.
  - **Freeze:** three chills freeze a creature solid, and the next blow shatters it for half again as much.
  - **Ink poison:** a creature beaten while poisoned bursts in an ink explosion that poisons those near it and leaves a slowing pool.
  - The red ribbon's fire burns and the blue ribbon's water chills. Some creatures shrug off what suits them (fire wisps don't burn, slimes don't mind ink).
  - **On Halgakos:** burn and poison drain a little but never take her below half a heart, chill slows her, and a stun is short and can't come again at once. Chips on screen show how long each lasts, and the right ribbon clears it.
- **Real effects, drawn frame by frame:** slashes, impact stars, ink splashes, sparkles, critical flashes and shield shatters on hits; a shockwave and dust for the ground pound; feathers for glide and aerial strike; flames, ice, poison clouds and stun stars on whatever is afflicted; and bursts when creatures are beaten.
- **Creatures fall their own way:** slimes splat, ink creatures melt, books fall apart into pages, paper crumples, golems crumble, frozen ones shatter and burning ones turn to ash.
- **Twelve new creatures for chapters 1 to 6,** built round the effects: the Cinder Sprite, Frost Folio, Venom Quill, Ink Bomb Beetle, Static Bookend, Glacier Tome, Flame Fan, Plague Page, Snowglobe Golem, Ashen Scribe, Smog Moth and Thunder Clapper, each with a word from Halgakos when she first meets it.
- Fixed: frozen creatures couldn't be hit, and hunts could count the newest creatures as the wrong kind.
