## Halgakos finds her voice

**"Sloppy~♡"** is her catchphrase now: her own name for you, closing whatever she says.

- Over a hundred lines of her own, teasing, smug and fond underneath: "Play with me Sloppy~♡", "C'mon little Sloppy~♡", "Counting on your fingers again Sloppy~♡", "Good Morning Sloppy~♡". She greets you by the time of day, dares you to her games, and now and then says something in Japanese.
- Her lines in the games and pranks are in the same voice, and she does not say the same thing twice running.
- "Where did my output go?!" is still what she cries when a game or a calculation goes wrong for her.

## Idle is prank time

Left to herself she now plays a prank every several minutes, announced with a "Watch this Sloppy~♡". They are still only a drawing over the screen, she owns up within seconds, and a tap ends one at once. **Surprise pranks** in her settings switches them off.

## Her idle animation no longer shrinks

The animated Halgakos used to narrow from the collar down as she idled. Her idle now keeps its own head, with the blink and the bob, over the steady body and moving tail of her working animation.

## Download

Download **Halgakos-Calculator-1.3.1.apk** below and open it on your Android device. It installs over 1.2.0 to 1.3.0.

Coming from 1.1.4 or earlier? It installs beside your old one, your saved calculations do not carry over, and the old one should be uninstalled afterwards. See the [1.2.0 notes](https://github.com/Shirochi-stack/coral-calculator/releases/tag/v1.2.0).

Requires Android 8.0 or later. This is a debug-signed preview build.
