## Two taps for the mini calculator

Opening the mini calculator now takes **two taps** on Halgakos, close together, so a stray touch no longer brings it up. One tap still closes it, and a hold still opens her menu.

The first couple of times you tap her once, she tells you: "Tap me twice for the calculator Sloppy~♡".

**Two taps to open it**, in her settings, is on by default. Switch it off to open the mini calculator with a single tap as before.

## A better Plus run

- **New art.** A painted sky with clouds, hills and floating islands that drifts by behind the land; turf and soil for the ground; ledges of stone; a plus that turns as it hangs there; minuses that walk on two feet and go flat with dizzy eyes when you land on them; a flag and a golden gate. The pictures were made with an image model through Glossarion and cut up for the game.
- **Her jump.** In the air she now shows her jump frames by where she is in the jump: leaving the ground, rising, at the top, coming down. She stretches as she rises and falls, flattens for a moment as she lands, and leans the way she is going.
- **More to feel.** A plus bursts into sparks when you collect it, a landing kicks up dust, a flattened minus puffs, and the view follows her smoothly, showing more of what is ahead than behind.

## Download

Download **Halgakos-Calculator-1.9.0.apk** below and open it on your Android device. It installs over 1.2.0 to 1.8.0.

Coming from 1.1.4 or earlier? It installs beside your old one, your saved calculations do not carry over, and the old one should be uninstalled afterwards. See the [1.2.0 notes](https://github.com/Shirochi-stack/coral-calculator/releases/tag/v1.2.0).

Requires Android 8.0 or later. This is a debug-signed preview build.

Only the APK is needed to install. `SHA256SUMS.txt` is available for optional download verification. The code of Halgakos Calculator is licensed under [MIT](https://github.com/Shirochi-stack/coral-calculator/blob/master/LICENSE), with a copy included below; the Halgakos artwork is not.
