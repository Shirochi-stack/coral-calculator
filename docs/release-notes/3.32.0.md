## Plus run: tougher creatures, hits you can feel

- **Colour tiers for every creature.** Each kind now has its own health, and tougher coloured versions appear as you go further:
  - Red: about 3 times the health
  - Purple: about 7 times, from land 7
  - Gold: about 15 times, glowing, from land 41
  - Black Ink: about 30 times, with a dark aura, from land 51

  Tougher ones hit harder, give more experience and drop more.
- **Hit feedback:** every hit flashes the creature white, knocks it back a little, splashes ink and pops up a damage number. Gold numbers mark great blows. Creatures that take more than one hit show a health bar in their colour. Heavy hits shake the screen slightly.
- **Beaten creatures clear away quickly,** gone about a second after they fall.
- **Halgakos's own book:** her starting weapon is now a pink and gold book with her silhouette on it. It throws glowing pink pages, and its special stamps her silhouette. The old plus-sign book can be found as a drop, the "Book of Addition".
- **Accessories look like jewellery:** brooches, pendants and earrings instead of shields. Item cards say whether a thing is a weapon, armour or an accessory.
- **A calm background:** the paper trees and meadow no longer jump with every step or hop, and the camera ignores small steps.
