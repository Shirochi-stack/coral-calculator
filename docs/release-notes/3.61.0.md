## Plus run: creatures that come for her

- **Creatures go after her**: once they see her, with a red "!", they come at her, step off ledges to follow her down and jump up to reach her, never into water, lava, spikes or a pit; fliers home in on her and overshoot. Those that fight close crouch and glow before they lunge; throwers and gunners aim at her.
- **They are fair about it**: never faster than she runs, they give up after a while or when she is far off, leave her alone in villages, rooms and behind doors, and nothing moves for the first moments of a land.
- **More of them**: lands from the second on have many more creatures (land 3 from 22 to 62, land 10 from 159 to 222), and dungeon floors more too, with nothing laid near the start or in a village.
