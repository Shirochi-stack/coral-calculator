## Plus run: break the shield

- **A boss's shield is broken by hitting it.** It no longer drops by itself after her attack. The shield is made of pieces: each blow on it takes pieces off, and when the last goes it breaks, she reels, and blows land on her, two at most, before it forms again. What she does herself never opens her; her pause after an attack is only the safest time to be at her shield.
- **A shield meter** under her health falls with each blow, with a small flame or drop for what the shield is weak to. The bubble cracks as it weakens, breaks with a flash and "Shield broken!", and comes round again as it forms.
- **What you hit it with matters.** A sword stroke, a hard landing, a book or steam takes two pieces; a book's burst three; the ink gun half a piece a shot. Fire bites harder on shields of paper, water on those of ink or fire. Brown's touch takes nothing off.
- **It grows through the fight.** Each third adds a piece, shortens the opening and quickens the forming; in the last third a shield that breaks throws her back.
- **Every boss:** the eight of the story, the seven between them, the Old Book and the three that roam. Early shields are two or three pieces; the Mawang's is six.

Bosses take a good many more blows than before, since none is free. If a fight is now too long, say which.

Page brawl is as it was in 3.6.2.
