## Plus run: a readable hint on the map

- **Halgakos's hint on the map** is written at its full size and wraps over as many lines as it needs. It used to shrink to fit one line, which on some pages left it too small to read.
- **Her words once every land is read** now say a hundred lands.
- **Groundwork for what comes after land 100.** The art and the workings of the story's last turn are in, and shut: land 100 ends exactly as it did in 3.19.0.

Page brawl is as it was in 3.8.3.

## Download

Download **Halgakos-Calculator-3.19.1.apk** below and open it on your Android device. It installs over 1.2.0 to 3.19.0.

Requires Android 8.0 or later. This is a debug-signed preview build.

Only the APK is needed to install. `SHA256SUMS.txt` is available for optional download verification. The code of Halgakos Calculator is licensed under [MIT](https://github.com/Shirochi-stack/coral-calculator/blob/master/LICENSE), with a copy included below; the Halgakos artwork is not.
