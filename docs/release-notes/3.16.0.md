## Plus run: the black market, the black arena, and four weapons to win

- **The black market** stands in one village in five from land 9, kept by Jupan as a side business.
- **Harder, by choice.** Three steps, taken and put back freely: each gives every creature one more blow to take and every hurt half a heart more, and she keeps a quarter more of the pluses she ends a land with. It holds from the next land begun.
- **The black arena.** A ladder of ten rungs: the Guild Guard, Penchok-jipsa, Illya, Tsubakieh, Zero, Byeoru, the Gazette Twins, Yeobaek, King Slime and the Mawang, each fought in her own place and stronger: more blows to take, more shield, and from the fourth rung a hurt that costs more. A rung opens when the one before is won and the story is past that boss. The prize is shown before the fight.
- **Prizes.** Pluses, the smith's materials, pet treats, four main weapons, and at the top a black sash that shows on the map. Every win pays tokens, and the market sells the ring of API keys, treats and materials for them.
- **Four main weapons, won in the arena,** each hers to begin a land with:
  - **The katana.** A tap draws, cuts further than it looks, and sheathes. Three taps in rhythm chain, the third the longest and heaviest. Held, she dashes about four and a half squares and everything on the line is cut a beat later. In the air it cuts downward.
  - **The fountain-pen lance.** A thrust through everything in front; held, a jet of ink further out.
  - **The ink-well cannon.** A slow lobbed ball with a wide burst; held, a great ball straight through everything.
  - **The ribbon whip.** A lash from her feet to well overhead; held, a spin that takes both sides.
- **Drawn for her.** Fourteen frames for the katana and twelve for each of the others, with their own effects, cards and three smith's levels. Hunts can ask for creatures beaten with each.
- **The smiths stand on a step,** clear of most of the buttons.
- **Four new lines,** three of Jupan's and one of Halgakos's.

Known: in a hair colour, the whip's red ribbon takes on that colour in places.

Page brawl is as it was in 3.8.3.
