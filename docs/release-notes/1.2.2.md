## Clearer companion settings

Two settings had names too alike to tell apart. They are renamed and grouped, with the mini calculator's own settings first.

**Mini calculator**

- **Close it when you tap away.** Touching anywhere outside the mini calculator closes it. On by default. (This arrived in 1.2.1 as "Hide the mini calculator when you tap away".)
- **Open it with a tap.** Turn off to have a tap open her menu and a hold open the mini calculator.

**Halgakos**

- **Keep her out of the calculator app.** Halgakos herself steps aside while the full calculator app is on screen. This is the setting that used to be called "Hide while the calculator is open"; it has nothing to do with the mini calculator.
- Animated Halgakos, only while unfolded, snap to the edge, fade when resting and her size are as before.

## Telling two copies apart

Since 1.2.0 changed the app's ID, a release from before it stays installed as a separate app with the same name and icon, its own settings and its own Halgakos. It is easy to open the wrong one.

- The calculator now says so when it opens, if an older copy is still installed, and takes you to Android's page for the old one so that you can uninstall it.
- The companion's settings show which version you are looking at, at the foot of the page.

## Download

Download **Halgakos-Calculator-1.2.2.apk** below and open it on your Android device. It installs over 1.2.0 and 1.2.1.

Coming from 1.1.4 or earlier? It installs beside your old one, your saved calculations do not carry over, and the old one should be uninstalled afterwards. See the [1.2.0 notes](https://github.com/Shirochi-stack/coral-calculator/releases/tag/v1.2.0).
