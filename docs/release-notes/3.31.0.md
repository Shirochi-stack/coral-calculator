## Games first

- **The app opens on the games.** The calculator is one tap away: the calculator button in the games screen's corner, or Back. If you had turned off "Open the app on the games", it stays off.
- **The games screen uses the whole screen** on phones, unfolded foldables and tablets, with Halgakos's picture taking less room.
- **The games button on the calculator stands out,** white on coral.

## Plus run

- **100 gallery pictures** of Halgakos, her friends and the people she meets, unlocked as the story goes on. The bad ending and the true ending each have their own.
- **The two films in four languages:** the prologue and the idol song can now be heard in Halgakos's voice in English, Japanese, Korean or Chinese, with captions in any of the four, or none. Set it where the voice is chosen in the gallery.
- **A Vanity tab at the guild counter,** to choose how Halgakos looks:
  - **Default:** her own look.
  - **Show gear:** her worn armour and accessory colour her outfit, with a shimmer for Halgakos Rose pieces.
  - **Vanity:** an outfit you've bought.

  Three starter outfits are in so far: Pajama Set, Gothic Dress and Knight's Tabard. More outfits, a vanity shop and ear and horn styles come later. Vanity never changes what she can do.
