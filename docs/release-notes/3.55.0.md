## Plus run: the Mirror Depths

- **A new dungeon after the story's end:** a tall standing mirror in land 99. The glass stays dark until Lucy has been beaten; then it opens onto the Mirror Depths, fifteen floors of a silver-and-violet crypt seen through the glass.
- **Her own shadows live there:** the Shadow Self mirrors her moves, the Hidden Reflection is all but invisible until she is close, and the Shadow Archer shoots from afar. Their touch and their shots blind her for a moment, darkness closing in around her before it lifts. The white ribbon keeps her safe from it.
- **At the bottom, the Imperfect Copy:** a pale Halgakos with elf ears where her horns should be, a single wing and a cracked mirror-shard sword. She fights in three phases with her shadows beside her, looks different on later dives (hollow and shadowy, red-eyed, pale bunny pink), and cracks apart when beaten. Her first clear gives a Halgakos Rose mirror-shard sword.
- **Voiced lore:** Halgakos going down, the Copy speaking in her voice made cold ("You call them sloppy copies. I was the first one."), and Halgakos's goodbye.
- **The counter's colour wares have their own pictures** now.
- Fixed: touching the button next to a dungeon stairway could close the game.
