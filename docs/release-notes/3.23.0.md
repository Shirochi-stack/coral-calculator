## Plus run: two animated films, and the Cringe gallery

- **The prologue,** a 90-second animated opening, plays the first time the Guild Guard is beaten in land 6, before the next land. Halgakos talks it through over the music.
- **The idol song,** "Sloppy☆Stage", 90 seconds of the five on stage as an idol unit, plays the first time the true ending is reached. It comes upright or wide to match how the phone is held when it starts.
- **Three sounds for each film,** chosen in the gallery: Halgakos (the default), the film's own song as it was made, and Halgakos's earlier version.
- **Captions,** on or off, for the films and the animated scene in land 100. English for now.
- **The Cringe gallery.** A new button on Plus run's map, Halgakos cringing, opens a gallery of the films, their storyboards and the cast sheets. Everything in it is locked until the story reaches it; a locked tile says roughly where it opens. Pictures open full screen and can be zoomed and dragged.
- **No pop-ups for what opens:** Halgakos says so herself when something new is in the gallery, and the button shows a small dot until it has been seen.
- A tap or Back ends a film, and the game carries on.

## Page brawl

- **The picked fighter says her line** as soon as the fighters screen opens, not only when a portrait is tapped.

## Also

- The animated scene in land 100 keeps its picture as it was drawn, with the line fitted to her mouth (from 3.22.3).

The films make the app about 52 MB larger.
