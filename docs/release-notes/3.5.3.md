## Page brawl: knocked down means on the ground

- **A fighter who is knocked down lies on the ground,** and no longer floats above it. The frames of her lying, knocked out, sitting up and getting to her feet were being placed by the lowest thing in the drawing, which for Halgakos was the keys hanging from her hand, so her body hung in the air above them. They are now placed by where her body rests. All five fighters are corrected; Halgakos was the furthest off.
- Her shadow, while she lies, is as wide as she lies.
- There is no drop or hop between the last frame of falling and the first of lying.

Everything else is as it was in 3.5.2.
