## Plus run: hits you can feel, and a real black ribbon

- **Every hit lands with a burst** in its weapon's style: a slash arc for blades, an ink splat for brushes, pens and guns, a star burst for blunt weapons, and shards for wands and staffs. The creature flashes white, squashes and recoils. Critical hits, shield breaks and finishing blows burst bigger with a ring, and big hits pause for a split second.
- **No more creatures standing in mid-air:** a creature knocked off a ledge, or left with nothing under it, now falls. If it falls into water or lava, it is gone, without leaving loot.
- **The black ribbon is a real curse now:** while it lasts, Halgakos runs slower and jumps lower, hits for less, finds half the pluses, and drips ink that costs a little health (never her last heart). Ink drips at the edge of the screen, and a black chip shows how long it has left. A white ribbon cleanses it. It still keeps the coloured ribbons away.
- **A new line from Halgakos** for the game's new name.
