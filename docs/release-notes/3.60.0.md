## Plus run: Aelwyn, curios and 128 named weapons

- **Aelwyn Pennyfeather**, a travelling elf merchant with a teal bow in her ponytail, has her wagon in the villages of the third, fifth, seventh, ninth and eleventh chapters: curios and rare gimmicks for sale, new every day, and her own voice.
- **Selling**: at her counter the Inventory sells, with a price on every piece, "Sell all junk" for the plain gear and curios not in use, and a lock on anything to keep.
- **48 curios** to find and carry, three at a time: more pluses, pluses from further off, a little helper that fetches them, flowers in her footsteps, dodges, crits and more.
- **128 named weapons**, one of every kind for every chapter, each with its own picture; from gold up each has a passive that changes how it is used, and a glow in its element's colour.
- **The villages** have a signpost with a word of lore, and paper butterflies.
- **Rooms off the land** (cellars, vaults and the rest) have stone, brick and plank ground instead of flat colour, and **the buildings have real floors** that run to the foot of the screen.
