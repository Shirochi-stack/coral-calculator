## Plus run: underwater

- **Swimming:** with the blue ribbon, Halgakos swims in deep water. Jump to rise, down to dive, the arrows to move along, and a press at the surface leaps her out. In blue she can also stand on water she lands on from above.
- **Holding her breath:** without the blue ribbon she can stay under for 8 seconds, shown by a bubble bar. After that she loses a heart every second until she gets out.
- **Ink bolts:** underwater, whatever she holds fires an ink bolt.
- **Lagoons:** at least 25 lands from land 10 on have a lagoon, a room of deep water with the blue ribbon at its door, coral, pluses and a chest on the bottom.
- **Four new swimming creatures** that never leave the water: the Squidscribe keeps its distance and shoots ink, the Pufferquill bursts into quills, the Bookeel darts, and the Jellylantern drifts.
- **Two new voiced lines** for Halgakos: one for swimming in blue, one for running out of breath.
- **Her moves take her form's colour:** the D4 move frames and the new swim frames are now coloured for whichever form she is in, instead of always pink.
- Room cards now name lagoons and hideouts.
