## The mini calculator no longer opens on top of Halgakos

The mini calculator could cover Halgakos when she stood away from the edge of the screen: near the fold of an open foldable, or in the middle of a phone. It now always opens to her left or her right, and so does her menu.

- On an open foldable it still keeps to her side of the fold, but only where it fits beside her there. Next to the fold it takes her other side, and crosses the fold before it would cover her.
- Where neither side has room, as in the middle of a phone's screen, she steps aside to make room and steps back when the mini calculator or menu closes. Dropping her there while the mini calculator is open does the same.
- Against the edge of the screen, the mini calculator and the menu now keep their gap from her, where they used to be pushed up against her.

## Download

Download **Halgakos-Calculator-1.2.6.apk** below and open it on your Android device. It installs over 1.2.0 to 1.2.5.

Coming from 1.1.4 or earlier? It installs beside your old one, your saved calculations do not carry over, and the old one should be uninstalled afterwards. See the [1.2.0 notes](https://github.com/Shirochi-stack/coral-calculator/releases/tag/v1.2.0).

Requires Android 8.0 or later. This is a debug-signed preview build.

Only the APK is needed to install. `SHA256SUMS.txt` is available for optional download verification. The code of Halgakos Calculator is licensed under [MIT](https://github.com/Shirochi-stack/coral-calculator/blob/master/LICENSE), with a copy included below; the Halgakos artwork is not.
