## Plus run: swarms and shields

- **Many more creatures:** lands now hold 20 times as many creatures as before at the start of the story, rising to 50 times by land 100. They gather in swarms on the ground and on every ledge, stacked up where there's little room, with quiet stretches between swarms. Land 99 holds over 2,000.
- **Swarm creatures give less each,** so levelling and loot stay sensible. The recommended level for each chapter has been re-measured.
- **Shielded creatures:** from land 6, some creatures carry a shield: a book cover, then steel, gold, and finally black ink in the last lands. A blow has to be strong enough to hurt it (your level, weapon, rarity and attack all count). A weaker blow bounces off with a clang and "Too weak!". Enough strong blows break it with an ink burst, and pips show how much is left. Coming down on a shielded creature from above gets past the shield.
- **It's harder.** Standing in a swarm at a low level costs lives quickly, so get stronger gear and levels.
