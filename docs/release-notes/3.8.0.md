## Plus run: pets

- **A pet goes with her and fights by itself.** From land 3 on one waits in every land, a little past the first flag, a different kind from land to land; and one waits in a cellar. Touch it and it is hers for the whole of the land, with no timer: through doors, rooms and the cellar, and through a life lost.
- **Five kinds:**
  - **The ink jar** floats at her shoulder and spits blots at the nearest creature, dazing what it does not beat.
  - **The kitten** runs behind her and pounces on what is close.
  - **The puppy** charges along the ground and knocks over everything it runs through.
  - **The duckling** floats, drops on what is in the air first, and every third drop its quack dazes all that is near.
  - **The monkey** lobs nuts, and fetches pluses and ribbons to her.
- **Floaters and runners.** The jar and the duckling keep in the air beside her; the kitten, the puppy and the monkey keep to the ground behind her, climb and drop as she changes level, and are beside her again if left behind.
- **What a pet deals with counts as hers.** Nothing hurts a pet.
- **Against a boss a pet only helps:** it chips a shield but never breaks one alone, and never lands the last blow.
- Its face shows under the pause button while it is with her, and Halgakos has a word the first time each kind joins.

Page brawl is as it was in 3.6.2.
