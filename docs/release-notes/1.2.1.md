## The mini calculator closes when you tap away

- Tapping anywhere outside the mini calculator now closes it, so it is out of the way as soon as you go back to what you were doing.
- Touching Halgakos does not count as tapping away: a tap on her still closes the calculator herself, and it follows her if you drag her with it open.
- Want it to stay open while you use the app underneath, as before? Turn off **Hide the mini calculator when you tap away** in the companion's settings.

## Download

Download **Halgakos-Calculator-1.2.1.apk** below and open it on your Android device. It installs over 1.2.0.

Coming from 1.1.4 or earlier? This is a different app to Android since 1.2.0 changed the app's ID: it installs beside your old one, your saved calculations do not carry over, and the old one should be uninstalled afterwards. See the [1.2.0 notes](https://github.com/Shirochi-stack/coral-calculator/releases/tag/v1.2.0).

Requires Android 8.0 or later. This is a debug-signed preview build.
