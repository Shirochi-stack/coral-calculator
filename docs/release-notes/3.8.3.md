## Page brawl: a smoother stance

- **Standing has eight frames where it had four.** Each fighter's stance was redrawn as one cycle of eight, drawn together so that the frames agree with one another, and it plays twice as fast, so the same sway takes the same time and moves more smoothly. For all five fighters, in a fight and where they are picked.
- **No change of size.** The eight are sized as one, with the tallest at her standing height, as the four were; what moves within the loop is her knees bending. Nothing scales or stretches a fighter.

Plus run is as it was in 3.8.1.
