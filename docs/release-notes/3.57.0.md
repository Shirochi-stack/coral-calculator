## Plus run: six more hidden bosses

- **Six new hidden bosses** wait in hideouts, one in each even chapter: the Overdue Notice (chapter 2), the Papercut Queen (chapter 4), the Gilded Bookworm (chapter 6), the Wax Seal Tyrant (chapter 8), the Footnote King (chapter 10) and the Last Librarian (chapter 11). Each has its own attacks and defeat, a word from Halgakos when she finds it, and its own Halgakos Rose piece the first time it falls.
- **Every hidden boss now has a chapter of its own:** the first six moved to the odd chapters, so the Erasure Moth now waits from land 8 in the first chapter.

Download **Halgakos-Queen-of-Sloppies-3.57.0.apk** below and open it on your Android device. It installs over Halgakos Calculator 1.2.0 to Halgakos: The Queen of Sloppies 3.56.0.
