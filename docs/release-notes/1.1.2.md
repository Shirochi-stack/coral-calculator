## Fixes

- **Mini calculator cut off at the bottom.** On some screens the last row of keys lost its lower edge. The rows now share the card's height exactly, so the whole keypad fits on every screen.
- **Fade slider cut off at the left.** At its shortest setting the slider's handle was sliced in half. It now has room at both ends.
- **Animated Halgakos stood still once she had faded.** On phones that change their refresh rate as you go, she kept waiting for her next breath and never took it. She now carries on breathing and blinking while faded, with a shorter pause between breaths.

## Changes

- **Halgakos in the calculator.** She now stands beside the full screen button at the top of the calculator. Tap her to open the companion's settings.
- **A proper close button.** The companion's settings have a red **Close menu ✕** button at the top, and another at the foot of the page.

## Update

Download **Halgakos-Calculator-1.1.2.apk** below and open it on your Android device. Install over your current version to keep history and memory; no uninstall is needed.
