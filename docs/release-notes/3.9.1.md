## Plus run: new voices, and five stuffed pets

- **Somi and Nabi have new voices,** each picked from thirty samples: Somi a tiny, excitable little sister of an adventurer, Nabi a squeaky kitten of a cat girl who says "nya". Every line of theirs is recorded again, and the two that were held back are recorded for the first time.
- **Five more pets,** stuffed dolls of the cast about half her height:
  - **Mini Chikuwa** runs along and lobs bread rolls, and hands Halgakos half a heart now and then when she is short of one.
  - **Mini Rune** runs along crying puddles of ink that slow what stands in them, flicks ink at what is close, and hides behind Halgakos when a boss is near.
  - **Mini Halgakos with black wings** flies and swoops at the nearest creature, teasing as she goes.
  - **Mini Halgakos with white wings** flies and fires a ray of light, and every so often a ring of light turns aside one thing thrown at Halgakos.
  - **Mini Halgakos** runs along and copies her: whenever Halgakos uses what she holds, the doll throws a small one of the same.
- Ten kinds of pet now come round the lands, and Halgakos has a word for each of the new ones the first time it joins.

Page brawl is as it was in 3.8.3.
