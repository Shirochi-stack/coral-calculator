## Larger text

Everything is bigger and easier to read without glasses: the numbers on the display, the labels on the keys, and the text in History, Settings, and the converters.

## Swipe long calculations

Long calculations no longer shrink to fit. The digits stay at full size with the newest ones in view; swipe the display sideways to see the earlier part.

## Typing cursor

A blinking coral cursor now follows the number you are entering, so it is always clear where the next digit will go. It disappears once you press **=** and returns when you continue the calculation.
