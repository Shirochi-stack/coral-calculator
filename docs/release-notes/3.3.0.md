## Plus run

- **Hearts and lives are two counts.** A hit costs hearts and nothing else: she flinches, cannot be hurt for a moment, and the fight goes on, the boss keeping its health and phase. When the hearts run out she loses a life and is back at the last flag, or the arena's door, with her hearts full. When the lives run out the land is lost. She starts with three lives; **Spare bookmarks** at the guild counter buys a fourth, fifth and sixth. A fall into a pit, lava or water puts her back on firm ground for one heart.
- **Quests are handed in.** Finishing a quest used to say nothing. Now it says so at once, a gold mark goes over whoever is owed the visit, and reaching her she thanks you aloud and hands the prize over as a moment of its own, with the guild's seal stamped. Not gone back to, she comes running at the gate. Quests pay by kind, and finishing every quest of a chapter earns a medal.
- **Bosses show their shields.** A bubble in the boss's colour stays up while blows are turned aside, flashes when struck, and flies apart when she can be hurt, with a gold arrow over her that blinks before the opening shuts. Her bar says Shielded or Open.
- **Boss prizes arrive when she falls,** rising out of her with a card and Halgakos's word, and a colour goes into her hair there and then.
- **Brown.** Nothing at arm's length hurts her: bodies, swings, slams and quakes are turned aside. Her touch beats any ordinary creature from any side, spikes and all. What is thrown or shot costs half a heart. White has the same.
- **Red and blue** throw their fire and water together with whatever weapon is in her hands.
- **Calling a colour.** The wait is ten seconds where it was eighteen, and it starts only when the colour ends. The button shows which it is: on, waiting or ready. A colour ending fades out of her hair rather than snapping back, and every picture of her in a land, the losing one included, is in the colour she wears.
- **The colour buttons are on the right,** in the bottom row beside the weapon, clear of her.
- **The stall** has one ribbon and one ink ball, two breads and two ice creams at each visit, and sells no ribbon while a colour is on.
- **More.** A range room and the Old Book under some cellars; doors up out of the way; real entrances for the seven mid bosses; a use for black once it is won, five seconds as an untouchable shadow; bosses a little sturdier now that one hit no longer ends the fight; rumours of someone ending translations.
- **The Guild Guard has a new voice,** low and stern, picked from fourteen.

## The companion

- **A colour of your own, and a glow.** Once all eight colours are won, the Hair colour setting opens a tenth round: mix a colour with three bars or type its hex code, and switch on a soft light about her in the colour of her hair.
