## Plus run: nothing stops play

- **No dialogue blocks the game.** The story at the start of a land, the tutorial included, was a card over the controls that had to be tapped through with the land frozen. It is now said in the small bubble at the top left, under the hearts, with the speaker's portrait, each line for as long as its voice takes and then the next by itself. She is in control from the first frame of every land.
- **The first land teaches by doing.** The arrows glow until she has moved, then the jump until she has jumped.
- **Winnings are a small card at the top left.** A chest's purse, a place found, a piece of a heart, a quest's pay and a roaming boss's prize no longer come up in the middle of the screen: what was won flies to a small card that slides in and goes by itself, with a plain, gold or violet rim by how rare it is. A quest handed in no longer holds the land.
- **A down button.** A round arrow between the left and right arrows. On a thin ledge it lets her down through it, and held, through every thin ledge below; in the air it brings her down faster. It never drops her through solid ground or onto spikes, lava or water.
- **The purple times sign has a defeat.** Beaten, it was still drawn walking. It is now knocked into the air, lies cracked on its back and fades. Two other kinds that lay flat for ever now fade as well.

Page brawl is as it was in 3.5.3.
