## Plus run: her own poses for every weapon's slam and aerial strike

- **Ground pound and aerial strike have their own animations for every weapon type:** for the slam, she winds up in the air, dives weapon-first, lands in an impact crouch and rises; for the aerial strike, she spreads both wings and strikes three times. Nineteen weapon types each have their own, and there is a fist pound when she holds nothing.
- **Fixed: a flat blue disc** that appeared with hammer and greatsword slams. It was drawn by mistake as a pen's ink burst.
- Fixed: holding strike after an aerial strike ran out no longer starts charging a special.
- Outfits are being drawn for the new poses; until then she wears her own look in them.
