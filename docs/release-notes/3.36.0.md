## Plus run: six new kinds of weapon, and found weapons in hand

- **Six new weapon kinds, each with a tap attack and a held special:**
  - **Greatsword:** a wide overhead swing; held, a spinning slam that hits both sides.
  - **Summoning staff:** ink spirits fly at the nearest creatures; held, a ring of them.
  - **Mace:** a smash that makes creatures reel; held, a whirl.
  - **War hammer:** a slam with a shockwave along the ground both ways; held, a leap and a quake.
  - **Wand:** a bolt of its chapter's element (fire burns, ice holds, lightning jumps, ink bursts); held, a fan of five.
  - **Scythe:** an arc in front and behind; held, a reaping spin, and its hook pulls creatures in.
- **Weapons you find can be used:** open a weapon's card in the Bag and choose "Take it in hand". Halgakos starts every land with it.
- **Rarity counts:** higher rarities hit harder, reach further and strike quicker.
  - Gold weapons glow.
  - Red weapons burn and crackle and shake the screen on hits.
  - Halgakos Rose weapons shift colour and shed petals.
  - Red ink guns are machine guns.
- **144 named weapons,** one of each kind for every chapter, plus a Halgakos Rose of each kind.
