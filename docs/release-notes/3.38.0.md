## Plus run: every weapon looks like itself

- **Found weapons show in Halgakos's hands exactly as their icons:** every one of the 156 designs (12 kinds, one for each chapter, plus a Halgakos Rose of each), in every frame: standing, running, jumping, attacking and the held special.
- **She grips them properly,** with her fingers over the handle, and fast swings leave a smear and a slash trail in the weapon's rarity colour. Real blades gleam.
- **Katanas** rest sheathed at her hip and come out as bare blades when she draws and cuts, each matching its own icon.
- **The scythe is a real crescent scythe now.** The old folded-paper one is its own weapon, the **Folded Page Scythe**: White or Blue only, a little weaker and slower, found in the first three chapters.
- **Small weapons are easier to see:** knives and pens are drawn larger.
- Her own starting weapons keep their own look.
