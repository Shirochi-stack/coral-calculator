## Plus run, grown into a whole game

Plus run is now the main game, at the top of **Play with Halgakos** and marked as recommended.

- **Thirty lands in five chapters.** Plus Plains, Minus Marsh, Times Towers, Divide Depths and Zero's Citadel, each with its own sky and ground. The lands get longer and ask for more: spikes, springs, ledges over long gaps, and walls only a spring will clear. A map shows them all, with the stars you have won.
- **A story, The Lost Output.** Someone divided by zero. Zero made off with her output through the equals gate, and she wants it back. She tells it on cards before the first land, at the start of each chapter and at the end.
- **Her voice.** Seventy-three lines, spoken: the story, a remark at the start of every land, and what she says when she is hurt, wins, loses, finds something or lands on a sign. Her words are on screen as well.
- **Sound effects** for jumps, pluses, landings, springs, flags, hits, the gate and the rest. A speaker button on the map, and **Sounds in Plus run** in her settings, turn sound off.
- **Things to fight with.** A pen and a knife to throw, a book to lob, a brush and a sword to swing, and an ink gun that fires while its button is held. They lie about each land in a ring of light, and a button for whatever she carries appears beside the jump.
- **Signs that fight back.** A times cannot be landed on. A divide hops. Some minuses stand and throw knives along the ground, and some divides shoot a dot from the top of their hop. The sword sends these back and the brush wipes them away.
- **Zero.** He waits in land thirty, takes three blows, lobs books at you when you keep your distance, and keeps the gate shut until he is flat. Nothing thrown gets through him.
- **Losing, properly drawn.** When she is hurt she is thrown into the air and tumbles away; out of hearts, she sits on the card dazed, then sulks and cries.
- **Stars.** One for finishing a land, two for most of its pluses, three for nearly all.
- **The whole screen.** The game, its map and its story cards fill the screen, with four round buttons along the bottom. A quick tap on a button is never missed.

The pictures, her jump and her losing frames were made with an image model through Glossarion on transparent backgrounds, and her frames were matched to her own sprites for colour and size. Her voice was recorded with the voice of the Halgakos AI project. The sound effects are synthesized.

She also has a dozen new lines recommending the game.

## Download

Download **Halgakos-Calculator-2.0.0.apk** below and open it on your Android device. It installs over 1.2.0 to 1.9.0.

Coming from 1.1.4 or earlier? It installs beside your old one, your saved calculations do not carry over, and the old one should be uninstalled afterwards. See the [1.2.0 notes](https://github.com/Shirochi-stack/coral-calculator/releases/tag/v1.2.0).

Requires Android 8.0 or later. This is a debug-signed preview build.

Only the APK is needed to install. `SHA256SUMS.txt` is available for optional download verification. The code of Halgakos Calculator is licensed under [MIT](https://github.com/Shirochi-stack/coral-calculator/blob/master/LICENSE), with a copy included below; the Halgakos artwork is not.
