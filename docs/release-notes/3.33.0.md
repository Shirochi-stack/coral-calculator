## Page brawl: a Dash button, ultimates that land, jump on the right

- **A Dash button (»):** dashes the way you're holding, or toward your opponent with no arrow held. Back plus Dash hops or air-dashes backward. Heavy during an air dash still gives the smash. Double-tapping an arrow still works too.
- **Ultimates can't be jumped out of:** once the flash starts, your opponent is held until the ultimate comes out, and ultimates now reach anyone in front of you, on the ground or in the air. Someone truly out of range can still dodge it, and a guard still blocks. Rune's flood now rises as a wave instead of rolling along the floor.
- **Jump moved to the right,** beside the quick and heavy attacks, so you can jump and attack with one thumb. Dash sits above Jump, and the guard sits above the arrows on the left.
- **Keyboard and mouse,** for Google Play Games on PC:
  - A and D or the arrow keys move.
  - W, Up or Space jumps.
  - S or Down guards.
  - J is quick, K heavy and L special.
  - Shift dashes, O dodges, and I or U is the ultimate.
  - Esc pauses.

  The pause card lists the keys once a keyboard is used.
- **Two-phone play** needs both phones on this version.
