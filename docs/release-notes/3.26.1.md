## Plus run: the gate as a portal, cleaner button hints

- **The "=" gate is a portal now:** a golden arch with the equals sign glowing in its keystone, filled with a swirl of white-gold, pink and violet. It stays dim and still until Halgakos passes it, then swirls and glows. The column of light is gone.
- **Warping:** she is drawn into the swirl with a flash, and comes out of the other gate's portal the same way.
- **Button hints:** a hinted button's own rim turns yellow and pulses gently, instead of a yellow ring drawn around it that overlapped the button next to it.

## Download

Download **Halgakos-Calculator-3.26.1.apk** below and open it on your Android device. It installs over 1.2.0 to 3.26.0.

Requires Android 8.0 or later. This is a debug-signed preview build.
