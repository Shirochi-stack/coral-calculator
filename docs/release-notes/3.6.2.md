## Page brawl: Tsubakieh's home run is a home run

- **Normal home run has an animation of its own.** It used to play like an ordinary swing. Now she calls her shot with the bat pointed at the sky and draws it far back during the announcement; the swing is a white arc with a starburst where the bat meets; she holds the follow-through with the bat round over her shoulder, then shades her eyes to watch it go.
- **The one who is hit goes like a ball:** up over the stage and far along it, smaller and tumbling the higher she goes, with a twinkle at the top, and comes down for the same damage as before.
- **On contact** the fight stops for a moment with a white flash, speed lines, a shake of the camera and a sound of its own: the crack of the bat, a rising whistle, a far cheer.
- **Guarded,** it chips as before and drives her most of the way across the stage.

Plus run is as it was in 3.6.1.
