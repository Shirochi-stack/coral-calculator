## Close app, from Halgakos's menu

The last entry in Halgakos's menu is now **Close app**, in place of **Hide Halgakos**. It closes Halgakos Calculator altogether: she leaves the screen, and the calculator leaves your recent apps.

Nothing is switched off, so she is back the next time you open the calculator. Your calculation, memory and history are kept.

To hide her and keep her hidden, drag her onto the ✕ at the bottom of the screen, or turn off **Show Halgakos** in her settings, as before.

## Download

Download **Halgakos-Calculator-1.2.4.apk** below and open it on your Android device. It installs over 1.2.0 to 1.2.3.

Coming from 1.1.4 or earlier? It installs beside your old one, your saved calculations do not carry over, and the old one should be uninstalled afterwards. See the [1.2.0 notes](https://github.com/Shirochi-stack/coral-calculator/releases/tag/v1.2.0).

Requires Android 8.0 or later. This is a debug-signed preview build.

Only the APK is needed to install. `SHA256SUMS.txt` is available for optional download verification. The code of Halgakos Calculator is licensed under [MIT](https://github.com/Shirochi-stack/coral-calculator/blob/master/LICENSE), with a copy included below; the Halgakos artwork is not.
