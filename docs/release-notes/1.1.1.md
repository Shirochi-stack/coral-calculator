## Animated Halgakos

The companion has a second form: the pink-haired Halgakos, who moves.

- She breathes and blinks while she waits, runs the way you drag her, hops when the mini calculator opens, and works alongside it while it is open.
- Turn her on with **Animated Halgakos** in the companion's settings. The still Halgakos stays the default, because she uses less battery.
- The animation rests between breaths and stops while the screen is off.

## Tap for the calculator, hold for the menu

- **Tapping** Halgakos now opens the mini calculator, and **holding** her opens her menu. In 1.1.0 it was the other way round.
- Prefer it as it was? Turn off **Tap for the mini calculator** in her settings.
- Her menu has a new entry, **Hide Halgakos**, beside dragging her onto the ✕.

## On from the start

- The companion is now switched on by default. The first time you open the calculator it offers Android's permission to display over other apps; she appears once that is allowed and you leave the calculator. Choose **Not now** and nothing changes until you switch her on yourself.
- **Snap to the edge** is now off by default, so she stays where you drop her. If you never changed it, she no longer slides to the side.
- The companion's settings have a **✕** to close them.

## Fading, and Android's notification

- She now fades a second and a half after you leave her alone instead of three and a half, and the fade itself is quicker. A new slider under **Fade when resting** sets the wait, from half a second to ten seconds.
- While she is on screen, Android posts its own notification that Halgakos Calculator is displaying over other apps. That notification, including the icon beside it, belongs to Android and cannot be changed by the app. **Turn off that notification**, at the bottom of her settings, opens Android's switch for it.

## Update

Download **Halgakos-Calculator-1.1.1.apk** below and open it on your Android device. Install over your current version to keep history and memory; no uninstall is needed.

Requires Android 8.0 or later. This is a debug-signed preview build.
