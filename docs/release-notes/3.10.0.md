## Plus run: the secret places, rebuilt

- **Places worth finding.** A stash or a passage behind a door is no longer one small room. Each is put together from pieces, differently for every land and every place: spikes hopped on ledges, towers of blocks, springs under shelves, crumbling runs, a ledge that rides across spikes, a locked door and its key, a bridge that a lever raises, bars that a plate lifts, creatures on guard, a wall only a spring gets her over. A stash runs seven to ten screens and a passage ten to twelve, where both used to fit in three or five. No piece comes twice in one place.
- **Eight insides.** A wine cellar, a map room, a flooded vault, the inside of a clock tower, a greenhouse at night, a printing room, a crystal cave and a treasure vault, each with its own walls, light and things standing on its floor. A land's two places never look alike.
- **Something at the end.** A stash's chest stands on a shelf to be climbed or sprung to, with a guard from the second chapter on, and one thing more by chance: something to help, a pet that waits, or a shrine that takes a heart from whoever has more than one and ties a ribbon on her. A passage has a chest by its far door and a ribbon up on its first high place.
- **Door marks.** A stash she has not been into shows a question mark over its door; once she has, it shows what it holds: a chest, a paw or the shrine's ribbon.
- **A chest is picked up.** It blinks white twice, lifts over her head shrinking as it fades, and its pluses fly to the count, in half a second. It no longer bursts in front of her. A prize's picture rises from over her head instead of across her.
- **Nothing stops for a place.** The card across the middle on entering a stash or a passage is gone: a banner at the top left says what the place is and what to look for.

What a player has found, opened and kept is as it was. The cellars and the game rooms keep their layouts; the game rooms have the new insides.

## The map

- **Continue.** The button under the lands now goes on with the furthest land she has reached: Start, Continue, or Play again once it is cleared.
- **The guild counter is up beside the sound button,** a pill with what she has to spend, and a dot on its corner when that is enough for something.
