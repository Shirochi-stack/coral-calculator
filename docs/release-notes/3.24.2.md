## Halgakos

- **Games first is on by default:** the tap on Halgakos that used to open the mini calculator now opens her games. The mini calculator is still in her menu, and the setting can be turned off in her settings. If you had already turned it off, it stays off.

## Download

Download **Halgakos-Calculator-3.24.2.apk** below and open it on your Android device. It installs over 1.2.0 to 3.24.1.

Requires Android 8.0 or later. This is a debug-signed preview build.

Only the APK is needed to install. `SHA256SUMS.txt` is available for optional download verification. The code of Halgakos Calculator is licensed under [MIT](https://github.com/Shirochi-stack/coral-calculator/blob/master/LICENSE), with a copy included below; the Halgakos artwork is not.
