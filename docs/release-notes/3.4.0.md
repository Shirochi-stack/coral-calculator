## Plus run: colours mix

- **Colours stack.** Calling a colour adds it to what she wears; it does not replace what is on. Each colour keeps its own power, has its own time on, and has its own wait, which starts when that colour ends: blue can be called while red is waiting.
- **Tap or hold.** A tap on the ribbon calls the colour shown. Hold it and every colour she owns opens out in a row, with the land paused: slide onto the ones you want, let go, and they all go on at once. It works with another finger on an arrow or the jump.
- **The arrow works, and has a button of its own** in the bottom row, left of the ribbon. In 3.3.0 it was a small badge inside the ribbon's own touch area, so a thumb on it pressed the ribbon instead.
- **A ribbon picked up in a land adds to what she wears** and never replaces it, even when she is already wearing as many of her own as she may.
- **Her hair is the blend** of the colours she wears, and the ribbon shows which state the selected colour is in: on, waiting or ready.
- **Named mixes with powers of their own:** Steam (red and blue), Comet (red and yellow), Tide (blue and green), Mirror (brown and purple), Gale (green and yellow), Ember (red and brown), and Prism for any three. A banner names a mix when it forms.
- **White is all seven worn together:** white hair, a halo, every power.
- **How many at once:** two to begin with. **Prism** at the guild counter raises it to three, four, five and then all seven; **Quick ribbons** shortens each colour's wait.
- **Black** of her own is five seconds as a shadow nothing can touch, worn with the rest.
- **The Shop button's word fits inside it;** the pluses to spend are in a pill of their own above it. Text in round buttons and plates now shrinks to fit.

Page brawl is as it was in 3.3.2.
