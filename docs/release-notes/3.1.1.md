## Page brawl: the tap battle

- **A countdown and a tug of war.** The time left is now only a number counting down. The bar is separate and shows who is winning: each fighter's colour fills from her side, and the line between them moves toward whoever is being out-tapped.
- **The winner takes the bar.** When time is up the whole bar fills at once in the winner's colour, and her special ultimate follows.
- **Her own colour.** Each side is drawn in its fighter's hair colour: the bar, her tap count and her name. When both sides are the same fighter, the right side is a shade apart.

Plus run and the rest are as they were in 3.1.0.
