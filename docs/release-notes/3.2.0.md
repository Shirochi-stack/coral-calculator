## Page brawl: smoother fighters

- **More frames for everything.** Each fighter goes from 102 frames to 169. Standing is a six-frame breath; walking forward has twelve frames where it had six, and walking back eight where it had four; the jump has eight frames in the air and two for the landing; guarding, turning, being hit and getting up all have frames between them.
- **Every blow winds up and follows through.** A blow used to go from the stance straight to the strike and back. Now there is a wind-up before it lands and a follow-through after, for both five-hit chains, air and dash blows, the launcher, the shove, the smash and the ultimate.
- The lean into a blow eases in and out, and the flicker while she cannot be hit is a soft pulse.
- Devices with little memory load the fighters at a smaller size.
- Two-device play needs this version on both devices.

## Jump back into a game

- **Home no longer throws a game away.** Leave Plus run, or a fight against her, with Home or the recent apps button, and it stops on its pause and waits. Halgakos says so in a bubble: tap the bubble to jump back in. **Back to Plus run** is also in her menu, and the game's own banner carries on from where it was. A game left waiting is kept for half an hour. A fight against another device still ends when it is left.

## Hair colour

- **Brown is the shade of Chikuwa's hair,** in place of the dark brown, for the companion and for the brown form in Plus run.
