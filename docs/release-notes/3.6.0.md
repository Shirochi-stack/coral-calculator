## Plus run: harder, with things in the air

- **Flying creatures from land 21.** Seven kinds, each first met in a land of its own: the comma moth that patrols at jump height; the caret swallow that marks her and dives; the ellipsis cloud that lets full stops fall, with a shadow where each will land; the asterisk that hovers, aims and fires, and later throws the black curse; tittles that fly in a line; the candle wisp that only water puts out; and the rime flake that only fire melts. They keep clear of villages and flags.
- **Three roaming bosses, met in the middle of lands.** The Margin Wyrm flies over the land more than once, dropping full stops, and can be beaten or driven off. Blot rises out of the ground, leaps and splits a slime off with every blow. The Bookmark Gecko comes down a ribbon, hangs head down, drops and lashes its tongue. None has to be beaten: each has a prize for whoever does, a piece of a heart among it.
- **Longer lands** in Volume Two, and in lands 21 to 30.
- **Colours answered.** A boss shuts off one of her colours when it changes; a slime drinks fire and grows; a falling full stop grounds a flier.
- **Black is a curse.** A black ribbon, or black thrown at her, takes off every ribbon she wears and stops her calling or picking up any for twice a ribbon's time. Her hair goes black and the ribbon buttons are barred, with the seconds shown. Black ribbons lie just past good ones from land 13. It is no longer a colour she calls; the Mawang's prize is now that black lies on her half as long.
- **No place gives a white ribbon.** Games and quests that gave one give a ribbon of an ordinary colour, two in Volume Two. White, as all her colours worn at once, has a faint glow and the tag "unmastered".
- A card on screen steps aside for a wave banner.

Page brawl is as it was in 3.5.3.
