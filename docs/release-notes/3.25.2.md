## Plus run: rooted trees, pets on pedestals

- **The paper trees stand on the ground:** they have tall trunks ending in spreading paper roots on a mound of earth and pages, and a meadow of low paper hills behind them. Over steps and pits, no tree hangs over the sky any more.
- **Every pet in the pet shop has its own pedestal:** a honey-wood plinth with a carved paw print, brass trim and a cushion in the pet's colour. The top row stands on a proper wooden shelf.

## Download

Download **Halgakos-Calculator-3.25.2.apk** below and open it on your Android device. It installs over 1.2.0 to 3.25.1.

Requires Android 8.0 or later. This is a debug-signed preview build.

Only the APK is needed to install. `SHA256SUMS.txt` is available for optional download verification. The code of Halgakos Calculator is licensed under [MIT](https://github.com/Shirochi-stack/coral-calculator/blob/master/LICENSE), with a copy included below; the Halgakos artwork is not.
