## Plus run: the ribbon gacha

- **A gacha parlour** stands in one village in six from land 5 on; land 8 has the first. Its machine gives a ribbon of one of the colours it holds, by chance, in a capsule that rolls out and opens. Machines up to land 20 hold four colours, up to land 40 five, and after that all six.
- **A roll costs pluses** from what she has to keep: 20, then 10 more for each roll in that land. The odds are written over the machine.
- **Timers stand still inside.** Every ribbon's timer, black's too, waits while she is in the room and runs again when she leaves.
- **Doubles turn into pluses,** half the roll's price, and after two doubles the next roll is always a new colour.
- **Every colour the machine holds, in one land, is white:** all of them at once for a while, the white she has not mastered, with its faint glow and no beam. This is the only early white.
- **A black ribbon blows the machine up.** It is shut for the rest of the land, every ribbon she wears is gone, and black lies on her for twice a ribbon's time from the moment she leaves.
- **Pots of black ink** stand in the lands from 41 on: a pot swells with a warning mark, then throws the black curse at whoever is near.
- **Two new lines** of Halgakos's.

Page brawl is as it was in 3.8.3.
