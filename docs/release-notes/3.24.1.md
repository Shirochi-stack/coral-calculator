## Plus run: the Contents Village, drawn properly

- **New ground** in every land of the Contents Village: a green lawn with small paper flowers on a path of paper-cobble stones, over layered brown soil with pebbles, roots and paper scraps. It replaces the flat pink strip.
- **A wood of paper trees** behind it: folded-leaf, round pink, umbrella and willow trees made of paper. They drift past more slowly than she runs, nearer and further ones by turns, behind everything in play.

## Download

Download **Halgakos-Calculator-3.24.1.apk** below and open it on your Android device. It installs over 1.2.0 to 3.24.0.

Requires Android 8.0 or later. This is a debug-signed preview build.

Only the APK is needed to install. `SHA256SUMS.txt` is available for optional download verification. The code of Halgakos Calculator is licensed under [MIT](https://github.com/Shirochi-stack/coral-calculator/blob/master/LICENSE), with a copy included below; the Halgakos artwork is not.
