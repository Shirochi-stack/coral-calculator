## Plus run: paper trees, crafting and the Halgakos Rose

- **Paper trees grow in the lands** from land 2: plain paper everywhere, fine parchment from chapter 4, and golden pages from chapter 8. Strike one with what she holds until it falls; it leaves a stump and gives its paper, which is kept at once.
- **The smithy has a Craft tab** inside the lands:
  - **Fold a paper weapon** of any of the 12 kinds from plain paper and pluses, or a blue one with fine parchment. Each paper weapon has its own icon.
  - **Hone** the found weapon in her hands up one rarity, white up to red.
  - **The Halgakos Rose:** an ultimate weapon for every class, made from golden pages, gold leaf, ink crystal and pluses, from level 60. Each can be made once.
  - **The Mother Tongue's Quill:** once all 12 Roses are made, at level 90. Every blow counts for 12, it reaches further, it glows rose-gold with letters drifting up, and holding it down sends out a wave that strikes every creature nearby.
- **Hunts can ask for paper trees** to be cut down.
