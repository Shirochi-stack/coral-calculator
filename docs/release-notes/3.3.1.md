## Plus run: the places behind doors

- **Bigger, and each for something.** Every place behind a door is taller and two to four times as wide as it was.
  - **A stash** is a climb: the chest is in sight from the door and not in reach, up steps that crumble or by springs to a shelf under the ceiling.
  - **A passage** is another way through the land, three screens long, over spikes on ledges, with a ribbon on an upper way and a chest by its far door.
  - **A cellar** has ledges at two heights to fight its three waves from, and in some lands the Old Book sleeps under the last of them.
  - **Mini games:** catching pluses, the range, and a new one, flatten them, where signs keep coming for fifteen seconds.
- **A title card on the way in** says what the place is, what to do and the count to beat, and nothing begins until it has been read.
- **A clock and a count while it runs:** the seconds as a number and a bar running down, her count against the count to beat, or the wave and how many are left. The last three seconds go red and tick.
- **A result card** when it is over, with what it gave.
- **Door marks** once a door is found: a chest, two arrows, a sword or a star, and a tick when it is done.
- **Rewards worth the detour:** a life from a cellar, the rare white ribbon for beating a game's count, purses that grow by chapter, and pieces of a heart from hidden stashes and the Old Book: four pieces make a heart.
- A hurt inside a room costs a heart and leaves her in it; only a life lost puts her out.
- Her lines about Plus run no longer say it has thirty lands.

Everything else is as it was in 3.3.0.
