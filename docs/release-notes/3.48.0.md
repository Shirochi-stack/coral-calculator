## Plus run: dungeons

- **24 dungeons,** two in every chapter, each reached by a stairway in its land. Walk up to one to see its floors, the level it's for, your deepest floor and its keeper, then go down.
- **Laid anew every time:** 5 floors in the first chapters, rising to 8 at the end. No two dives are alike, and the floors get longer and harder the deeper you go. Hearts and lives carry down with her.
- **A blessing after every floor:** pick 1 of 3 for the rest of the dive: a heart more, harder blows, more pluses, a life more, all hearts mended, or hurts that cost less.
- **Floor kinds:** gilded floors pay double, teeming floors hold twice the creatures, lightless floors are dark except around her, quiet floors are calm with bread, and fierce floors have tougher creatures.
- **Six keepers** guard the bottom floors: the Card Catalogue, the Bookbinder Beetle, the Inkwell Kraken, the Gargoyle Lectern, the Kite Dragon and the Hollow Archivist. Each fights in three phases and drops gold-or-better loot. The first clear of a dungeon gives its relic and triple pluses.
- **Seven new dungeon creatures:** the Inkblink Moth, Shelf Spider, Folio Frog, Quill Wisp, Bouncing Stamp, Anvil Tome and Ink Double.
- **Ten new voiced lines** for Halgakos.
- **On the map:** a stairway mark on each dungeon's land, a star once it's cleared, and how many of the 24 are cleared.
