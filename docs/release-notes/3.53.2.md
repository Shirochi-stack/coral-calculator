## Plus run: ground pound, glide and a new aerial strike

- **The down button is on the right now,** between the strike and jump buttons, so one thumb can press down while attacking.
- **Ground pound slams with what she holds:** hold down in the air (even out of an air dash) and she drops and smashes the ground with her weapon, knocking up and dazing what is near. Three levels at the counter: a wider, harder slam that takes more shield pieces, and at the top level, shields broken outright and a shockwave running along the ground. A ring, cracks, dust and flying bits in her weapon's colours show its reach.
- **Glide has three levels:** each falls slower, and the top level gives a rising gust as she glides.
- **Aerial strike, a new move at the counter:** in the air, tap strike for her normal attack, or hold it to hang in the air with her wings spread and strike everything around her. Three levels make it last longer, hit faster and reach wider.
- **New skills in the tree** for these moves: a ring round her seed (Heavy Descent, Updraft, Hang Time, Sky Dancer), the Skyborne keystone, and Quake Slam, Meteor Fist, Sky Sigil, Falling Star and Swallow Dive for their classes.
- Her own pose art for the new moves comes in a later update; for now she uses her nearest existing poses.
