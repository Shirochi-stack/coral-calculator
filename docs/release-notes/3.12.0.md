## Plus run: pets of her own, and a pet shop

- **Pets to keep.** The guild counter has a Pets tab with the ten kinds on show. A pet bought there is hers for good; the ones chosen to come along walk into every land with her and are never changed for a pet she finds.
- **Strays.** A pet found in a land still goes with her for that land. With no room left, it stays where it lies and a Swap button over the jump lets it take the place of the stray that has been with her longest. Her own pets are never touched.
- **Up to six.** One place for a pet of her own and one for a stray to begin with; the counter sells up to three of each. Those that run line up behind her, those that fly hang behind her shoulder, none on her and none on another, and they take turns so that no two begin on something at once. Their faces are under the pause button, with a dot on those that are hers.
- **The kennel.** Brisker pets wait less between one thing and the next; stronger pets count twice on a big creature, then take a whole piece off a shield. A pet still never breaks a shield or lands the last blow.
- **The counter in tabs.** Hearts, Moves, Colours, Comforts, Pets and Arms, with a dot on a tab when she can afford something under it. Colours lists the mixes: one she has made is named with what it does; one she has not shows one of its colours and a question mark. Everything already bought is as it was.
- **Back closes the backpack** before anything else, on the map, in a land and at the counter.

Prices, in pluses: the ink jar and the kitten 300, the puppy and the duckling 350, the monkey 400, Mini Chikuwa and Mini Rune 600, the winged Mini Halgakoses 700, Mini Halgakos 800.

Page brawl is as it was in 3.8.3.
