## Plus run: twelve villages, people to talk to, homes with secrets

- **Twelve villages, one per chapter:** Contents Village, Hushwick, Rosemarrow, Cairn Index, Endleaf, Gildcrest, Inkwell Row, Margin Ford, Goopvale, Footnote Hollow, Dog-Ear Bay and Colophon.
  - Each has its own landmark, an entrance arch with its name, and every building in one street: guild hall, pet shop, smithy and a home, plus the gacha and the market once they've opened.
  - The arch is a portal too, so warping to a village puts her right under it.
  - The other places that used to be villages are now homesteads: one home, a quest-giver at the door, a stall, and a well or a paper tree.
- **Talk to people:** tap anyone you've met (villagers, Nabi, Inju, Moru, Altteul, Nuri). They say something about where the story is, in their own voice, and Halgakos answers. Some lines point you to quests. A "…" bubble shows who has something new to say.
- **Homes:** six furnished interiors: a kitchen with a sleeping cat, a reading nook, an attic bedroom, a workshop, a greenhouse and a music room.
- **Secrets:** most homes hide one: a false wall with a hidden room and a chest, a glinting loose floorboard to press down on, or a diary page left out. There are 18 diary pages of villagers' lore to collect.
