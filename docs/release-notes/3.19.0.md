## Plus run: land 100, and Lucy, Dragon of Lost Words

- **Land 100 is open,** and Lucy waits at its end. Plus run is now a hundred lands.
- **Lucy on the ground,** in three parts with a shield to break each time. First as a child with a water gun, a swim ring she throws and catches, and water balloons; then with her dragon side out, on the wing, with mist that falls the length of the place and a swoop.
- **The black ribbon.** Her last part is not fought on the ground: she throws the black ribbon and everything stops. What happens next depends on whether Halgakos has every colour and every one of the nineteen friends' pledges.
- **With all of them,** the ribbon turns white on her, her friends come round her, and she takes off after Lucy as true white.
- **The flight over the library,** seen from behind her. The arrows steer; holding strike gathers the white beam, and only the beam reaches the dragon. Mist, stars, gusts and pillars come back along it, each with a mark where it will land. Three stages, with her friends reading along at the end of each.
- **Without all of them,** the land is unmade. It is watched, not played, it is not counted as a loss, and a card afterwards says which colours and which pledges are still missing and where to find them.
- **A life lost** on the ground starts that part again, not the whole fight; a life lost in the air starts the flight again.
- **Drawn for it:** Lucy as a child, with her dragon side out and as a dragon, and new frames of Halgakos's own for the beam, from the side and from behind. Her white wings beat faster and shed feathers.
- **Sixteen new lines:** ten of Lucy's, two of Halgakos's and four that close the land.

The story has one more turn to come after land 100.
