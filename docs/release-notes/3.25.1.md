## Plus run: the village rooms, tidied

- **The pet shop's button says "Buy"** (or "Pets" for one she already has) and opens the pet's card to confirm.
- **Prices are never hidden:** they sit on small tags, under the feet of the pets on the floor and over the heads of those on the shelf.
- **Nabi faces into her shop** from behind her own counter by the door, with treat jars and a bell.
- **New drawings:** the guild's board of hunts, a desk for Inju, and the smiths' anvil, in place of plain shapes.
- **No buttons over anything:** in every building the floor sits above the on-screen buttons, so none covers a pet, a price, a keeper or a smith.
