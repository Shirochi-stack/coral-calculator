## Plus run

- **The map on its side has big lands**: a scrolled page of chapters with lands about twice the size, dragged and flung, opening at the chapter she has reached; the wheel and the arrow keys scroll it too.
- **Coins that spin and shine**: +1, +5, +25 and +100 plus coins, a creature's pluses falling in the biggest that fit, with a tenth more; a gold coin from a great keeper and a prismatic one from a hidden one.
- **Glossary gems**: every boss drops one, and others now and then; for fifteen seconds her pluses count double, and more gems add more time.
- **Halgakos's own shape** on the dash and ground pound buttons.
- **Every outfit has the ground pound and the air attacks**, for every weapon.

## Fixes

- **After a prank she runs home** to where she stood, instead of being back there at once when the screen is touched.
