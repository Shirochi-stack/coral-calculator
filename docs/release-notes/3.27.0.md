## Plus run: lands in layers

- **Every land but the last has caves below it**, and high ledges above it where the sky is clear. Both are laid out fresh each time you play, so a replay finds new caves, ledges and treasure. The main way through each land stays exactly as it was.
- **Going down:** a glinting hatch in the ground drops her into a cave with the down button. A pit over a cave becomes a secret way down instead of a lost life.
- **Going up:** vines outdoors and ladders indoors. Hold jump to climb, down to go down, and let go to hang on. Springs bounce her up to hidden ledges.
- **Caves** have uneven floors, ledges of pluses, the land's creatures and a treasure chest. A vine or ladder at the far end climbs back out, so the layers join up again. Each chapter has its own underground.
- **Hidden areas:** many caves end in a false wall that looks like rock until she's right next to it, with a bigger chest and a ribbon or treat behind it. Above the high ledges, a spring leads to a hidden top ledge with a chest of its own.
