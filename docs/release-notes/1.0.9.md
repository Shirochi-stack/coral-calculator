## Tapping after equals no longer changes anything

In 1.0.8, tapping a finished calculation or its answer switched to editing at once. A tap on the answer removed the calculation above it, so a stray tap on the way to a long-press copy could leave you typing into the answer.

A tap after **=** now only places the cursor where you tapped. The calculation and its answer stay as they are until you press a key.

- With the cursor in the gray calculation, the next key brings the calculation back for editing at that spot.
- With the cursor in the answer, the next key edits the answer itself as a new number. The finished calculation stays in History.
- A key that has no effect at the cursor, such as a second decimal point, leaves the finished calculation in place.
- Long-pressing to copy the result removes the cursor again, so the next digit starts a new calculation as usual.
- **=** and **AC** ignore the cursor and work as they always do after equals.
