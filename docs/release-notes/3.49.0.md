## Plus run: the smith sisters' quests

- **A Quests tab at the smithy,** wherever all three sisters work it, from land 13. The tabs now read Moru, Pulmu, Sutdol, Craft and Quests.
- **Each sister has a quest of three steps,** built from what she already does: flattening creatures, guild hunts, metal, paper trees, dungeons and hidden bosses.
  - **Moru's lost hammer** gives a red Heartflutter Hammer.
  - **Pulmu's coat of scales** gives a red Heartflutter Index Scale.
  - **Sutdol's weighed twice** gives a red Heartflutter Hourglass Pendant.
  - Each also pays 300 pluses.
- **The three anvils:** once all three are done, the sisters ask together. It ends with clearing The Inkwell Depths and gives the Halgakos Rose Colophon Signet and 1,500 pluses.
- **Progress counts from when a step is taken up,** and metal is taken when it's handed in.
- **20 new voiced lines,** in the sisters' own voices, with Halgakos answering.
