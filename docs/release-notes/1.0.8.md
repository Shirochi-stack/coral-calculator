## Edit after equals

After pressing **=**, tap the gray calculation above the answer to bring it back for editing, with the cursor where you tapped. In 1.0.7 a finished calculation could not be tapped.

Tap the answer instead to edit the answer itself as a new number, with the cursor at the digit you tapped. The finished calculation stays in History.

## Long calculations on unfolded screens

On an unfolded foldable or a tablet held sideways, a long calculation now continues on further lines instead of running off the edge. Lines break before an operator where they can, and a number too long for one line breaks after a comma.

When there are more lines than fit, swipe up and down. Part of the next hidden line stays in view and fades out, so you can see there is more. Tap any line to edit there.

A long answer wraps as well, so its leading digits are no longer hidden.

Phones, cover screens, and phones held sideways keep a single line that swipes sideways.
