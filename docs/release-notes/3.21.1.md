## Plus run: Lucy's own voice

- **Lucy has the voice chosen for her.** All seventeen of her lines are recorded again in it, and the sounds in them are made, not read out: her "Bleh!" is a tongue stuck out, her "Ow!" a yelp. The text on screen is as it was.
- **"Splash!" is a sound, not a word.** She says "You're all wet, dummy!" and the splash is heard with it; she now says it only with her water gun, her balloons and her mist, so the two always come together.
- **The true white's ribbon button** no longer shows a number where a colour's seconds go: it has no clock, and shows a star.

Page brawl is as it was in 3.8.3.
