## Plus run: homes, new buttons, a lighter app

- **Eight homes in the villages**, one for each of her friends, each with its own room inside and a keepsake to walk up to: look at it, hear what Halgakos makes of it and what its owner says back, and find all eight.
- **A dash button** over the pound with the right thumb (beside it on a wide screen), once the roll or the air dash is hers; lit when a dash would go, dim when it would not.
- **A ground pound button** that comes up while she is in the air, once the pound is hers.
- **The down button** is back over the arrows.
- **In a dungeon, the floor is its number alone**, large and in gold at the top; how deep it goes is left to find out.
- **Waiting pets** no longer jitter on the ground as the view moves.

## Page brawl

- **See-through glass buttons** with white marks, as in Plus run; the ultimate's ring is gold.

## Fixes

- **Turning the screen during a game Halgakos opened over another app** now fits the game to the new shape, instead of leaving it the old width with the home screen showing beside it.
- **Halgakos opens the app's screens in its one task**, so the game stays in the recent apps instead of being reachable only through her.
- **Films, the gallery's pictures and new voice lines** now come in install-time packs of their own, so the app's base is less than half the size it was.
