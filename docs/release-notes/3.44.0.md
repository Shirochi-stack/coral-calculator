## Plus run: new moves, new creatures, fairer swarms

- **Eight new moves at the guild counter,** each pictured by Halgakos doing it:
  - **Triple jump** (needs wings): the third jump is a flip.
  - **Air dash:** in the air, double-tap a direction, or press Shift or L, to dash once per jump. Its second level makes the dash strike what it passes through.
  - **Dodge roll:** the same input on the ground rolls her, unhurt while she rolls.
  - **Wall jump:** push into a wall to cling and slide down slowly; jump to kick off it.
  - **Ground pound:** hold down in the air; landing strikes everything close by.
  - **Glide:** hold jump while falling to float down slowly.
  - **Thorns:** up to three blows back on whatever hurts her.
  - **Second wind:** once per land, a blow that would finish her leaves her half a heart. Halgakos has a new voiced line for it.
- **24 new unique creatures** across the story, each drawn on its own. Some carry their own shields.
- **Fairer swarms:** swarms are walkers only, stay near where they start, stack less high, keep away from pit edges, and their touch costs half a heart. Recommended levels are re-measured.
- **The guild counter scrolls** on every tab, the new Moves list included.
