## Two players in Page brawl

- **2 players here.** Each player has half of one screen and a set of buttons of their own. Best on a tablet or an open foldable, with the screen on its side.
- **Host a game / Join a game.** Two people on the same Wi-Fi play each other, each on their own device. One taps Host, the other Join; the devices find each other and talk straight to each other. No account, no server, and nothing is sent but the game. This is the first thing in the app that uses the network, so it now asks for network access, and the [privacy policy](https://github.com/Shirochi-stack/coral-calculator/blob/master/PRIVACY.md) says exactly what is sent.

## Page brawl looks better

- **Sharper fighters.** Their pictures are kept half as large again, so they are no longer stretched soft on a big screen.
- **Three new stages.** The top of the Book at golden hour, Heart-Flutter Plaza at dusk, and the Silent Archive by lantern light, in place of the two pale ones.
- The word on the ultimate button fits inside it.

## New voices for Chikuwa, Rune, Illya and Tsubakieh

In both games. Each has a higher, younger anime voice near Halgakos's own register: Illya a bright magical girl, Rune tearful and fragile, Chikuwa and Tsubakieh light and cute. Every one of their lines is recorded again, and Page brawl's now with the scene of each line told to the voice, as in Plus run. Chikuwa no longer says "nyam". Halgakos's voice is as it was, but her first line in Contents Village is said at her usual pace.

## Your birthday

**Settings › Your birthday** takes a day and a month. When it comes she celebrates with you: a cake with a candle, a party popper, a party horn and confetti, with frames drawn for it.

## Fixes

- Going home, or to recent apps, now closes a game that covers the screen, instead of leaving it over the home screen.
- She no longer changes size by a few pixels when a prank begins.

Still to come, and being built: the fifty new lands of Plus run, with dialogue shown in the game beside a portrait of whoever speaks, ledges she can walk under, the slash drawn where the blade is, a clearer shut gate, and her hair-colour forms.

## Download

Download **Halgakos-Calculator-2.3.0.apk** below and open it on your Android device. It installs over 1.2.0 to 2.2.0. For a game between two devices, both need this version.

Coming from 1.1.4 or earlier? It installs beside your old one, your saved calculations do not carry over, and the old one should be uninstalled afterwards. See the [1.2.0 notes](https://github.com/Shirochi-stack/coral-calculator/releases/tag/v1.2.0).

Requires Android 8.0 or later. This is a debug-signed preview build.

Only the APK is needed to install. `SHA256SUMS.txt` is available for optional download verification. The code of Halgakos Calculator is licensed under [MIT](https://github.com/Shirochi-stack/coral-calculator/blob/master/LICENSE), with a copy included below; the Halgakos artwork is not.
