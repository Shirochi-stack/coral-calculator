## Plus run: the people of the world come alive

- **Every villager, friend, smith and keeper has their own idle animation:** blinking, glancing around, and habits of their own.
  - Inju stamps forms and flips her ledger.
  - Nabi flicks an ear, swishes her tail and has a good cat stretch.
  - Moru taps her hammer, Boksun sniffs her pie, Galpi strokes the snake on her staff, and Pulmu threads a needle.
  - Sutdol counts coins, the Guard salutes, and Yeobaek nods off.
- **Nobody moves in step:** each runs on their own clock.
- **They stand perfectly still where they stand:** only the drawing changes, with no bobbing or shaking.
