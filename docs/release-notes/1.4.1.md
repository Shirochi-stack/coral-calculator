## Her tail swings

The animated Halgakos's tail hardly moved while she idled: what changed from frame to frame was its colour, dusky to bright and back, which looked like a light playing on it. It is now one drawing in one colour, and it swings: a flick to one side and back from where it comes out behind her skirt, then still while she rests.

## She does not fade while she is talking

- **A speech bubble brings her back to full strength** if she had faded.
- **She stays that way for as long as the bubble is up**, however short the time before she fades is set.
- Once the bubble has gone she waits the usual time and fades again.
