## Plus run: hideouts and hidden bosses

- **Hideouts:** 28 lands, about one in three from land 8, hide a secret hall halfway along. Get in by breaking a crack that only a strong blow can break, or through a door on a high ledge. An unbroken entrance glints.
- **Six hidden bosses** wait at the far end of the hideouts, one for every two chapters: the Erasure Moth, Madame Quillon, the Last Line, the Typeset Golem, Marginalia and the Unwritten. Each fights in three phases with its own shots.
- **Their loot:** a weapon, an armour and an accessory, all gold or better and themed to the boss. The first time each boss falls, Halgakos also gets its own Halgakos Rose piece.
- **On the map:** a found hideout shows a crack on its land, and a beaten hidden boss a gold star.
- **Two new voiced lines** for Halgakos: near a hideout, and on beating a hidden boss.
- **More depth:** pieces of the scenery pass in front of her, the far sky moves slower and the near trees faster, and the layered lands have one more raised section.
- **Swarms** stay off the highest ledges and come in smaller groups.
