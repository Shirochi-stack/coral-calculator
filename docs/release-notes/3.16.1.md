## Plus run: more in the air in the later lands

- **More creatures as lands go on.** The later lands hold about 5 to 10% more than they did, almost all of it in the air: fliers added to a land now grow from twice to five times its own with the land's level, and may fly closer together when they keep apart in height. Across both volumes there are 11,856 creatures where there were 11,118; the fliers of Volume Two went from 1,639 to 2,169.
- **The ground is as full as it was.** Packs keep the room to come down among them, and where a land had no more free ground it gained nothing there. The first two chapters are unchanged.
- **The whip's ribbon stays red** in every hair colour, and her neckerchief with it.

No land's ground changed.

Page brawl is as it was in 3.8.3.
