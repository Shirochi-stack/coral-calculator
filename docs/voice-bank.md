# Voice bank

Every voice designed for the Vampire Merchant, kept on the Inworld account so they can be given to other characters. Ids, design prompts and per-line pitch are in `tools/voice_bank.json`; sample clips are in `assets/voice-bank/<round>/` (not packaged into the app). Pitch is the median of voiced frames; Halgakos measures about 271 Hz by the same method. To give one to a new character, add its id to `VOICES` in `tools/make_run_voice.py` under a free letter, with its lift as the pitch.
