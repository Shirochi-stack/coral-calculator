# Google Play listing

What is entered in Play Console for Halgakos Calculator (`com.halgakos.calculator`). The graphics are in this folder; `python tools/make_art.py` rebuilds the icon and the feature graphic.

## Store listing

**App name** (30 characters at most)

```
Halgakos Calculator
```

**Short description** (80 characters at most)

```
A clean offline calculator, with Halgakos floating over your other apps.
```

**Full description** (4,000 characters at most)

```
Halgakos Calculator is a simple, offline calculator with large round keys and answers that update as you type. Halgakos, its mascot, can float over your other apps with a mini calculator in hand, so you can work something out without leaving what you are doing.

EVERYDAY CALCULATIONS
• Add, subtract, multiply and divide, with percentages and decimals. Multiplication and division are done before addition and subtraction.
• See the answer while you type. After =, the calculation stays above its answer.
• Tap anywhere in a calculation to edit it there. Backspace after = brings the calculation back.
• Memory keys, and a history of your last 100 calculations.
• A unit converter for length, mass and temperature, and a currency converter that uses a rate you enter.
• Large, readable digits with thousands separators. Dark appearance, full screen and landscape.

HALGAKOS COMPANION
• A mini Halgakos floats over your other apps. Tap her for a compact calculator, hold her for her menu, and drag her anywhere.
• The mini calculator and the app share one calculation, so either carries on where the other left off.
• Choose the still Halgakos or her animated, pink-haired form, who breathes, blinks and runs when you drag her.
• Settings for her size, for fading when she rests, for snapping to the edge of the screen, and more.
• Turn her off whenever you like: drag her onto the ✕, or use her menu.

MADE FOR FOLDABLES
• The keys widen to fill an unfolded screen. Held sideways, the display and the keypad sit either side of the fold.
• The companion can stay away while the device is folded and return when you open it.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Works entirely offline. No ads, no accounts, no analytics and no network permission.
• Your calculations stay on your device.

PERMISSIONS
• Display over other apps: used only to draw the companion and her mini calculator, and only after you allow it.
• Run at startup: so that a companion you have switched on returns after the device restarts.

Open source: https://github.com/Shirochi-stack/coral-calculator
```
