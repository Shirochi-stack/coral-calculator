# Google Play listing

What is entered in Play Console for Halgakos: The Queen of Sloppies (`com.halgakos.calculator`). The graphics are in this folder; `python tools/make_art.py` rebuilds the icon and the feature graphic.

## Store listing

**App name** (30 characters at most)

```
Halgakos: Queen of Sloppies
```

(The game's own title is "Halgakos: The Queen of Sloppies". Play allows 30 characters, so the listing drops "The". Play still shows "Halgakos: Sloppy Copy" until this is sent for review.)

**Short description** (80 characters at most)

```
A smug catgirl translator fights sloppy copies in a 100-land story RPG.
```

**Full description** (4,000 characters at most)

```
A book fell from the sky, and the world of translation is full of sloppy copies: careless rewrites, misprinted pages and words twisted out of shape. Halgakos, a smug catgirl translator with a sharp tongue and a soft spot for mint ice cream, sets out to fix every last one of them. Sloppy~♡

PLUS RUN: A STORY RPG
• 100 lands across three volumes, from Contents Village to the Dragon's Library, told through the story of the webtoon "A Book Fell from the Sky".
• Lands run into one another, with caves below, high ledges above, hidden rooms behind false walls, and portals to travel between the places you have found.
• Twelve villages with shops, smiths, a pet shop and a guild counter. Talk to the people you meet: their lines change as the story goes on, and some lead to quests.
• Levels with no cap, rare loot in six rarities up to the shimmering Halgakos Rose, armour and accessories that change how you fight, and pets that follow you into battle.
• Creatures come in tougher colours as you go further, and every boss has its own fight, ending in a finale against the Mother Tongue herself.
• Fully voiced: Halgakos and her friends speak every line.
• Animated films and the Cringe gallery: unlock films, storyboards and pictures as the story moves on. Voices in English, Japanese, Korean or Chinese, with captions in any of the four.

PAGE BRAWL
• A fighting game with five fighters, each with her own moves, combos, air dashes and ultimate.
• Four difficulties, from Easy to Brutal, and two-player fights between two phones on the same Wi-Fi.

MORE GAMES WITH HALGAKOS
• Tic-tac-toe, rock paper scissors, pairs, quick sums, chess, Last card, Minesweeper, Number rain, Bubble count, Catch Halgakos, and her pranks.

HALGAKOS ON YOUR SCREEN
• Halgakos can float over your other apps. Double tap her for the games, hold her for her menu, drag her anywhere. Turn her off whenever you like.

A CALCULATOR, TOO
• The app began as a calculator, and it is still one tap away: answers that update as you type, memory keys, history, and unit and currency converters.

MADE FOR FOLDABLES, TABLETS AND PC
• The games screen fills unfolded foldables and tablets, and the app runs on Google Play Games on PC.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No ads, no accounts and no analytics. Nothing you do is collected or shared.
• The network is used only for two-player Page brawl between devices on your own Wi-Fi.

PERMISSIONS
• Display over other apps: only to draw Halgakos over your other apps, and only after you allow it.
• Run at startup: so that a companion you have switched on returns after the device restarts.
• Network: only for two-player Page brawl on your local Wi-Fi.

Open source: https://github.com/Shirochi-stack/coral-calculator
```
