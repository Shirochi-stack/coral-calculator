# Halgakos Calculator privacy policy

Effective 25 November 2026.

Halgakos Calculator does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data. It has no accounts, ads or analytics, and it does not have the permission to use the network.

## What stays on your device

- Your current calculation, the value in memory and your last 100 completed calculations.
- Your settings, including where the Halgakos companion was last placed.
- Your tallies and best results in the games played with Halgakos.

This is kept in the app's private storage. It leaves the device only if you copy a result yourself. Clear saved calculations from the **History** dialog, or remove everything by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it.

## Permissions

- **Display over other apps** is used only to draw the Halgakos companion, her mini calculator, her speech bubbles, her games and her pranks on top of other apps, and only after you switch her on and allow it in Android's settings. The app does not read, record or send what other apps show, or what you type or touch outside its own windows.
  - A game that is played all over the screen, which you start yourself, covers the screen while it is up, so touches go to the game until you leave it. The app underneath is dimmed, not read.
  - A prank is a drawing on top of the screen and nothing more. It cannot see, delete or change anything in another app or on your home screen. The icon she appears to delete is the calculator's own. For the few seconds it lasts, a touch ends the prank instead of reaching the app underneath. She plays one every several minutes while idle, and **Surprise pranks** in her settings switches those off.
- **Run at startup** is used only so that a companion you have switched on returns after the device restarts.

The app can also see whether its own earlier edition, the one released before the app's ID changed in version 1.2.0, is still installed. It uses this only to remind you to remove the duplicate. It cannot see which other apps you have.

Switching the companion off in **Settings › Halgakos companion**, or withdrawing the permission in Android's settings, removes her from the screen.

## Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects nothing from anyone.
